# JOURNEY ATLAS — Top Hero Art Brief

Updated: 2026-08-23
Status: production brief

## Purpose

The top Hero set establishes the visual benchmark for JOURNEY ATLAS before full destination-card production. The Hero must feel like a travel atlas illustration, not a photograph and not a loose watercolor painting.

## Locked visual direction

- Real named place.
- Painterly realism: approximately photo 60 / illustration 40.
- Clearly illustrated on inspection.
- Natural light and atmosphere.
- Refined painterly simplification; no obvious watercolor wash.
- No HDR / stock-photo look.
- No collage.
- No text, labels, flags, logos, frames or UI inside the image.
- Geography, architecture, vegetation and landmark relationships must remain believable.
- Horizontal composition suitable for the top Hero image.
- Each Hero should differ strongly in landscape, palette and travel motive while still sharing the same rendering language.

## Production set — 5 visuals

### HERO 01 — Patagonia / Argentina

**Real place:** Laguna de los Tres / Mount Fitz Roy, Los Glaciares National Park

**Scene:** Fitz Roy catching first warm light above the Patagonian basin, with the lake and rocky foreground anchoring the composition.

**Travel motive:** Earth / dramatic landscape

**Light:** dawn

**Palette:** cool stone, blue water and restrained warm alpenglow

**Avoid:** exaggerated red mountain glow, impossible lake reflections, fantasy peaks.

---

### HERO 02 — Petra / Jordan

**Real place:** Al-Khazneh (The Treasury), Petra

**Scene:** The Treasury emerging from the Siq opening, with a small human scale element and soft reflected sandstone light.

**Travel motive:** History / architecture

**Light:** early morning

**Palette:** sandstone rose, muted shadow blue, warm neutral ground

**Avoid:** crowded tourist scene, oversized camels, invented façade details.

---

### HERO 03 — Okavango Delta / Botswana

**Real place:** Okavango Delta

**Scene:** A small elephant group crossing shallow delta water through reeds with open floodplain and distant tree line.

**Travel motive:** Wildlife / landscape

**Light:** late afternoon

**Palette:** soft green, dry gold, reflected sky blue

**Avoid:** safari-poster animal crowding, impossible mixed-species grouping, dramatic storm HDR.

---

### HERO 04 — Aitutaki / Cook Islands

**Real place:** Aitutaki Lagoon

**Scene:** Luminous shallow lagoon, pale sandbar and one or two small motu islands under a broad tropical sky.

**Travel motive:** Sea / island

**Light:** clear daylight

**Palette:** turquoise, pale sand, deep Pacific blue, restrained tropical green

**Avoid:** generic resort advertising, over-saturated cyan, invented mountains.

---

### HERO 05 — Registan / Uzbekistan

**Real place:** Registan Square, Samarkand

**Scene:** The three madrasas forming a coherent architectural space in warm evening light, viewed from a believable pedestrian perspective.

**Travel motive:** City / history

**Light:** late afternoon into early evening

**Palette:** warm stone, blue tile, soft sky

**Avoid:** fantasy ornament, excessive night lighting, empty geometric poster composition.

## QA gate

A Hero passes only when:

1. The source place is immediately plausible and recognizable.
2. The image is clearly part of the same ATLAS art family as the other four.
3. It does not read as a literal photograph.
4. It remains visually strong at the deployed Hero crop.
5. It introduces a distinct travel motive from the other Hero images.

The user should only be asked to choose if two materially different visual directions survive internal QA. Routine scene/crop refinements do not require approval.
