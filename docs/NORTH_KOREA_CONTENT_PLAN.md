## Page concept

**NORTH KOREA / 北朝鮮**

Lead direction:

平壌の計画都市と大同江から、白頭山の火口湖、金剛山の花崗岩峰、開城の高麗史、妙香山の寺院、七宝山の海岸地形、高句麗古墳、黄海側の大規模水利、そして軍事境界線へ。政治的イメージだけで国を語らず、朝鮮半島北部の山地・火山・海・古都・宗教文化・近現代史が同じ国土に重なっていることを見せる。

Editorial stance:
- present landscapes, history and lived geography without promotional language about the state or its political system
- do not aestheticize military power, leadership imagery, mass rallies or propaganda
- where travel access is discussed, distinguish clearly between geographic interest and whether ordinary tourism is actually possible
- Japanese travel guidance must reflect the current Ministry of Foreign Affairs request to refrain from travel at implementation time

## Hero

### H — Pyongyang / Taedong River and Ryugyong Hotel skyline

Working coordinate: **39.030, 125.755**  
Planned filename: `hero-pyongyang-taedong-ryugyong.webp`  
Content ID: `pyongyang-taedong-ryugyong`

Visual identity:
- real Pyongyang skyline viewed across or along the Taedong River
- the triangular Ryugyong Hotel silhouette must be clearly recognisable at natural scale as the city anchor
- broad river foreground and layered residential / civic skyline should establish Pyongyang as a planned capital, not a single-building portrait
- low surrounding hills may appear on the horizon where geographically plausible
- soft clear morning or late-afternoon daylight; restrained pale concrete, blue-grey river and muted urban greens
- enough open water / sky for Hero copy and enough skyline mass to survive mobile crop
- no mass rally, military parade, missiles, flags, leader portraits, propaganda poster treatment, giant monument montage or invented neon skyline
- no readable generated Korean or English text
- photo 6 : quiet watercolor 4; refined painterly realism consistent with Iceland / Norway
- one continuous 3:2 horizontal frame only; no collage, panels, border, map, title, caption or UI

Why this Hero:
- the Ryugyong Hotel silhouette plus Taedong River makes the capital immediately legible as Pyongyang without relying on a political spectacle
- it opens with a real urban landscape, while the eight Scenes deliberately move into the country's mountains, heritage and coast

## Locked 8-scene selection

### S01 — Mount Paektu / Heaven Lake

Working coordinate: **42.006, 128.057**  
Planned filename: `mount-paektu-heaven-lake.webp`  
Content ID: `mount-paektu-heaven-lake`

Scene identity:
- real Heaven Lake filling the volcanic summit caldera on Mount Paektu
- rugged grey-brown crater rim and deep blue lake dominate the composition
- elevated rim viewpoint at physically plausible hiking/viewing height
- late summer or early autumn with only restrained residual snow if seasonally plausible
- no Himalayan snow exaggeration, fantasy volcano eruption, Chinese-side tourism structures as a focal point or giant flag

Why:
The caldera landscape establishes the volcanic highlands at the northern edge of the peninsula and is visually unlike every urban or coastal Scene.

### S02 — Mount Kumgang / granite peaks and valley in autumn

Working coordinate: **38.650, 128.070**  
Planned filename: `mount-kumgang-granite-autumn.webp`  
Content ID: `mount-kumgang-granite-autumn`

Scene identity:
- real Mount Kumgang landscape with pale near-white granite pinnacles, forested valley and rocky stream
- broad valley-level or modestly elevated view; recognizable jagged granite mass must dominate
- restrained autumn foliage in red, ochre and green
- natural mist only if it does not obscure the geology
- no fantasy spires, Chinese shanshui exaggeration, temple montage or poster-sunset saturation

Why:
UNESCO inscribed Mount Kumgang – Diamond Mountain from the Sea as a mixed World Heritage property in 2025. The Scene adds granite geology, seasonal forest and a long cultural association with mountain Buddhism.

### S03 — Kaesong / historic tiled-roof city and Songak mountain context

Working coordinate: **37.970, 126.555**  
Planned filename: `kaesong-old-city-songak.webp`  
Content ID: `kaesong-old-city-songak`

Scene identity:
- elevated real view across Kaesong's historic urban fabric with clusters of grey tiled roofs and low-rise streets
- Mount Songak / surrounding basin hills must remain visible to explain the old capital's mountain-ringed geography
- heritage architecture and living city context together; not a single reconstructed gate portrait
- soft spring or early-autumn daylight
- no invented palace skyline, generic South Korean hanok village, flags or readable signage

Why:
Kaesong adds the Koryo-period capital layer and shows how historic urban form sits inside a mountain basin rather than isolating one monument.

### S04 — Mount Myohyang / Pohyon Temple

Working coordinate: **40.020, 126.320**  
Planned filename: `mount-myohyang-pohyon-temple.webp`  
Content ID: `mount-myohyang-pohyon-temple`

Scene identity:
- real Pohyon Temple courtyard with Korean tiled roofs, restrained dancheong and mature trees
- Mount Myohyang's forested / rocky slopes visible as setting where plausible
- architecture remains human-scale; one or two small visitors only if natural
- clear summer or early-autumn daylight
- no giant palace proportions, invented dragons, ceremonial crowd, leader imagery or tourist-poster saturation

Why:
The Scene connects mountain ecology with a Buddhist site whose history reaches back to the Koryo period, adding a religious-cultural layer distinct from Kaesong.

### S05 — Mount Chilbo / Sea Chilbo cliffs

Working coordinate: **41.064, 129.629**  
Planned filename: `mount-chilbo-sea-cliffs.webp`  
Content ID: `mount-chilbo-sea-cliffs`

Scene identity:
- real Sea Chilbo coast in North Hamgyong with steep weathered volcanic / igneous rock cliffs dropping into the East Sea
- dark blue water, forested cliff tops and layered coastal headlands
- broad elevated coastal viewpoint at plausible height
- no Mediterranean or tropical turquoise substitution, fantasy sea arches or exaggerated vertical scale

Why:
Chilbo adds the far northeast and an eroded rocky coast. It is on the DPRK's UNESCO Tentative List as of 2026.

### S06 — Nampo / West Sea Barrage

Working coordinate: **38.720, 125.200**  
Planned filename: `nampo-west-sea-barrage.webp`  
Content ID: `nampo-west-sea-barrage`

Scene identity:
- real long concrete barrage crossing the Taedong estuary near Nampo
- repeating sluice / causeway geometry across broad water, with low forested hills and estuary context
- elevated but plausible land-based viewpoint
- structure shown as landscape-scale infrastructure rather than heroic propaganda
- no ships enlarged into a military subject, flags, banners, fireworks or poster text

Why:
The western estuary provides a human-shaped water landscape and makes the Taedong system visible beyond the capital.

### S07 — Complex of Koguryo Tombs / grassy burial mound

Working coordinate: **38.950, 125.430**  
Planned filename: `koguryo-tombs-grassy-mound.webp`  
Content ID: `koguryo-tombs-grassy-mound`

Scene identity:
- real Koguryo tomb landscape: one principal grass-covered earthen mound with a simple stone entrance / approach if appropriate to the selected tomb group
- pine trees and low foothill setting; broad enough to read as a burial landscape rather than an archaeological close-up
- no cutaway interior, exposed mural reconstruction, fantasy warriors, grave goods or museum display

Why:
The DPRK's first World Heritage property preserves a major pre-Koryo history layer. The exterior mound also creates a quiet visual counterpoint to the capital and mountain Scenes.

### S08 — Panmunjom / Joint Security Area

Working coordinate: **37.956, 126.677**  
Planned filename: `panmunjom-joint-security-area.webp`  
Content ID: `panmunjom-joint-security-area`

Scene identity:
- real Panmunjom Joint Security Area with the small blue conference buildings aligned around the Military Demarcation Line
- frontal or slightly oblique ground-level composition with architecture and boundary space dominant
- only a few personnel if naturally required for scale; no weapon close-up or confrontation scene
- subdued overcast or clear daylight
- no combat, explosions, dramatic standoff, propaganda banners or invented flags

Why:
The border is not a conventional scenic attraction, but the division of the peninsula is essential geographic and historical context for understanding the country.

## Travel / access editorial rule

At implementation, current conditions must be rechecked against Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs guidance and other high-confidence current sources.

As of the Content Plan date, Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs Overseas Safety website states for all of North Korea: **「渡航を自粛してください。」** and says the request applies regardless of purpose. The Country Page must therefore not present a normal independent-travel itinerary as though it were currently actionable.

Keep the standard Country Page structure, but frame Travel Scale / Transport / Travel Notes as contextual information for periods and arrangements in which lawful organised access is available, while clearly surfacing current Japanese guidance. Do not invent operating border crossings, independent rail booking, card/payment availability or unrestricted photography rules.
