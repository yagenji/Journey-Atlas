# MACAU — content plan

Updated: 2026-09-09  
Branch: `country/macau`

This document stores editorial and visual-design intent for JOURNEY ATLAS Macau. It does not connect unapproved visual assets to production and does not change `atlasPublished:false`.

## Page concept

**MACAU / マカオ**

Lead direction:

聖ポール天主堂跡の石段から、中国寺院、ポルトガル風の広場、丘の灯台、タイパの家並み、コロアンの海辺、干拓で生まれたコタイへ。小さな地域を歩くと、交易港の歴史と現代の都市改造が近い距離で重なっている。

The page should reveal Macau through:
- the Historic Centre as an urban network rather than a list of monuments
- Chinese religious and maritime culture that predates the Portuguese settlement
- Portuguese and Chinese architecture occupying the same walkable street fabric
- hills, Inner Harbour / southern waterfront and the Pearl River estuary as real geography
- Taipa and Coloane as former island communities with different scale from the peninsula
- Cotai as reclaimed land that physically changed the map
- Macanese food as a maritime cultural mixture, not simply “Portuguese food in Asia”
- contemporary Macau beyond casinos: transport, public space, residential districts and street culture

Editorial boundary:
- Do not frame Macau as a sovereign state. It is a Special Administrative Region of the People’s Republic of China.
- The Country Page template is reused operationally, but copy should use “地域”, “マカオ”, or “特別行政区” where political-status wording matters.
- Do not invent a capital. Macau has no separate national capital; the visible common fact should state this explicitly.

- people may appear only at small natural scale; avoid a dense tourist crowd

Historical anchor:

## Travel Scale

Macau is compact, so the duration thresholds must not be inflated to match larger countries.

### 1日 / city
**旧市街を一本の都市ルートとして歩く**

BarraのA-Ma TempleからLilau、Senado Square、Ruins of St. Paul’s方面へ、Historic Centreを建物単位ではなく街路の連続としてたどる。  
例：媽閣 → リラウ → セナド広場 → 聖ドミニコ教会 → 聖ポール天主堂跡／モンテの砦。

### 2〜3日 / map
**半島からタイパ・コロアンまで地図を広げる**

旧市街に加え、Guia Hill、Sai Van、Taipa Houses、Cotai、Coloane Village、Hác-Sáを日を分けてつなぐと、歴史地区だけでは見えない地形と土地利用が分かる。  
例：マカオ半島 → タイパ／コタイ → コロアン村／黒沙海灘。

### 4日以上 / compass
**小さな地域を急がず見た後、珠江河口へ旅をつなぐ**

博物館、丘、住宅街、食、海岸まで時間を使い、マカオ内を「短時間で消化」しない旅にする。さらに日数があれば、定期フェリーや港珠澳大橋、陸路を使い香港・珠海へ広げると、珠江河口の都市関係まで地図で理解できる。  
例：マカオ各地区 → 香港、またはマカオ → 珠海。

The final bucket is intentionally open-ended and may extend beyond Macau after the territory itself has been explored. Do not imply that conventional highlights require four days.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - temperature and humidity rise through spring; mist / rain becomes more common
   - walking remains practical, but outdoor plans should include rain flexibility
   - avoid promising uniformly clear skyline conditions

2. **6〜8月**
   - hot, humid and rainy; heavy rain and tropical-cyclone conditions can disrupt outdoor movement
   - use early / later parts of the day for exposed streets and hills where conditions allow
   - current SMG warnings take priority over evergreen seasonal copy

3. **9〜11月**
   - humidity and rainfall generally ease as autumn progresses, making long urban walks easier
   - tropical cyclones can still affect the region, especially earlier in the period
   - do not describe the entire period as typhoon-free

4. **12〜2月**
   - cooler and generally drier under the northeast monsoon
   - hilltop / waterfront areas can feel cooler and windier than dense streets
   - mild subtropical winter does not mean every day is warm; cold surges occur

## Primary Themes

- **city / 街を歩く**
- **history / 時をたどる**
- **food / 食をめぐる**

Rationale:
