## Page concept

**MOLDOVA / モルドバ**

Lead direction:

石灰岩を刻むラウト川の谷から、葡萄畑と地下ワインセラー、ドニステル沿いの城塞、北部の丘陵、南部ガガウズの暮らし、プルト川の湿地へ。大きな山や海ではなく、農地・川・村・文化が近い距離で重なる東ヨーロッパの国。

The page should reveal Moldova through:
- Orheiul Vechi as a landscape where geology, settlement, archaeology and Orthodox heritage coexist
- Chișinău as a green, low-rise capital rather than a monument collage
- the Dniester / Nistru as the eastern river axis, shown through both fortress and landscape
- the country's agricultural surface and unusual northern hill landforms
- wine culture through a real underground limestone gallery, not vineyard advertising
- wooded valleys and monasteries as part of central/eastern Moldova
- Gagauzia as a distinct living cultural region in the south
- Lower Prut / Lake Beleu as Moldova's wetland and bird habitat

Editorial balance:
- 1 archaeological-landscape Hero
- 1 capital scene
- 1 medieval fortress / river scene
- 1 unusual agricultural hill landscape
- 1 underground wine landscape
- 1 wooded monastery valley
- 1 northern Dniester landscape
- 1 living cultural courtyard
- 1 southern wetland

Do not select a place simply because it is famous. Do not add Transnistrian-region scenes to this production set; current travel conditions and administrative complexity make them a poor fit for the core discovery sequence.

Planned filename: `hero-orheiul-vechi-raut.webp`

Scene identity:

- the embedded river meander, steep pale limestone banks, cultivated valley floor and village must read together
- the Orthodox church / cave-monastery area may be visible as a modest human anchor, never oversized
- late spring or early autumn, with restrained green / ochre agricultural colour
- broad horizontal composition with river geometry carrying the eye through the frame
- enough quieter sky / distant valley for Hero copy, without reducing the site to an empty panorama
- mobile crop must retain river bend + limestone ridge + one recognizable built anchor
- current JOURNEY ATLAS production style: photo 60 / illustration 40, refined painterly realism, visibly illustrated on inspection
- no literal stock-photo look, no obvious watercolor wash, no fantasy cliffs, no exaggerated canyon depth

Why this Hero:
- it combines Moldova's low-relief geography, limestone geology, agriculture, settlement and history in one real landscape
- the Răut meander is visually stronger and more country-specific than a generic vineyard or isolated church
- UNESCO describes Orheiul Vechi as a roughly amphitheatre-shaped archaeological landscape carved by embedded Răut meanders through limestone, with human occupation from prehistory through medieval periods
- it introduces the country as a landscape to understand, not as a list of wineries

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **赤と白の糸で春を迎えるマルツィショール**
   - theme: LIFE
   - The 1 March spring practices shared with Romania, Bulgaria and North Macedonia were inscribed by UNESCO in 2017.
   - The red-and-white thread is made, offered and worn, then removed according to seasonal signs such as first blossom / swallow / stork.

2. **壁掛け絨毯は、模様と技術で地域の記憶を残す**
   - theme: LIFE
   - Traditional wall-carpet craftsmanship of Romania and Moldova was inscribed by UNESCO in 2016.
   - It links weaving technique, household use, ceremony and regional motifs; today it continues through families, craft centres, schools and museums.

3. **南部にはガガウズの言語と暮らしが続く**
   - theme: LIFE
   - Gagauzia is an autonomous territorial unit with a distinct Gagauz cultural layer.
   - Use Congaz / Gagauz Sofrasi to explain domestic architecture, woven textiles and cuisine rather than reducing the region to ethnicity statistics.
   - Any demographic percentages must come from final 2024 census data at implementation.

4. **ワインは4地域をめぐる「道」になっている**
   - theme: ROAD / FOOD
   - Moldova Travel identifies Codru, Valul lui Traian, Ștefan Vodă and Divin as four historical wine regions.
   - Moldova's Wine Road has been part of the Council of Europe's Iter Vitis network since 2020.
   - Focus on landscape / producer / cellar continuity rather than brand rankings.

5. **世界遺産は、建物ではなく地球を測った測量点**
   - theme: HISTORY
   - Moldova's currently inscribed World Heritage property is the transnational Struve Geodetic Arc.
   - The Moldovan station at Rudi is one of the 34 listed original station points across 10 countries.
   - It connects the atlas itself to the history of measuring the size and shape of the Earth.

6. **農村の宿では、庭と食卓が旅の一部になる**
   - theme: LIFE
   - Moldova Travel currently presents a network of 50+ family-run rural B&Bs / guesthouses and highlights home-cooked meals using garden produce.
   - Use this as an observable travel pattern: orchards, kitchens, courtyards and hosts connect the agricultural landscape to daily life.

## Travel Scale

### 2〜3日 / city
**キシナウを入口に、オルヘイと地下ワインへ**

首都を歩き、Orheiul VechiとCricovaのどちらか、または移動を整理して両方へ広げる。短い滞在でも都市、石灰岩の谷、ワイン文化という三つの基準をつかめる。  
例：キシナウ → Orheiul Vechi → Cricova → キシナウ。

### 4〜5日 / map
**北か南を一方向だけ加える**

中央部にSoroca / Saharnaなど北東部を加えるか、Gagauzia / Lower Prutなど南部へ向かう。公共交通でも可能な区間はあるが、複数の農村景観をつなぐなら車やドライバーで余白をつくる。  
例：キシナウ → Orheiul Vechi → Saharna → Soroca → キシナウ。

### 7日以上 / compass
**北のドニステルから南のプルト湿地まで縦断する**

Naslavcea、Suta de Movile、Soroca、中央部、Gagauzia、Lake Beleuまで広げると、約350 kmの南北幅の中で丘陵、河川、農村文化、湿地の変化を追える。  
例：キシナウ → Suta de Movile → Soroca → Naslavcea → Orheiul Vechi → Congaz → Lake Beleu → キシナウ。

The final duration is intentionally open-ended. Do not compress all eight scenes into a rushed 4–5 day route merely because the country is geographically compact.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - Mărțișor begins the cultural spring in early March.
   - orchards, fields and village landscapes become greener; walking at Orheiul Vechi / central valleys fits naturally into this period
   - spring weather can still vary; do not promise fixed temperatures in production copy

2. **6〜8月**
   - long daylight supports rural / north-south driving days
   - summer can become hot, especially in exposed fields and city streets; use morning / evening for open-landscape scenes
   - wetland and bird conditions are seasonal and should not be promised as a specific species encounter

3. **9〜10月**
   - strongest overall travel-season direction for vineyards, harvest landscapes and food / wine culture
   - National Wine Day is normally held on the first weekend of October; exact annual dates must be rechecked
   - early autumn also gives Suta de Movile, Orhei and Dniester hills more readable colour / light

4. **11〜2月**
   - shorter daylight shifts the trip toward Chișinău, underground wineries, museums and monastery architecture
   - rural transport / guesthouse schedules require more planning
   - winter landscape can be quiet rather than reliably snowy; do not market Moldova as a snow destination
