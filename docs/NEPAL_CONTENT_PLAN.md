## Editorial direction

Nepal is not treated as an “Everest-only” destination. The page begins with the Himalaya, then deliberately expands south, east and west to show the country’s vertical and cultural range: Himalayan high mountains, Newar urban heritage, a lake basin, the rain-shadow plateau, Tarai wildlife, the Buddhist birthplace at Lumbini, eastern tea hills, a remote high-altitude lake and a lived-in trekking town.

Primary themes:
- `earth` — the elevation and climate gradient is a defining reason to travel.
- `road` — trekking makes movement itself part of the destination.
- `history` — Kathmandu Valley and Lumbini make religious/urban history a primary layer, not a side note.
