## Page concept

**BELARUS / ベラルーシ**

Lead direction:

赤煉瓦の城館から、氷河が残した湖群、原生林、ネマン川の古都、南の湿地へ。平坦な国土を進むほど、水と森の静かな広がりに中東欧の歴史が重なる国。

The page should reveal Belarus through:
- Mir as the recognisable Hero rather than a generic capital skyline
- Białowieża / Belovezhskaya Pushcha as old-growth lowland forest and European-bison habitat
- the glacial lake mosaic of Braslaw
- Grodno as a Neman River historic city
- Nesvizh as a landscaped aristocratic residence distinct from the fortified Mir Hero
- Minsk as a river-edge capital rather than a collection of monuments
- the Pripyat floodplain / Polesie wetland landscape
- Polotsk as an old eastern river city
- Brest Fortress as a visible 20th-century historical layer

Editorial balance:
- Hero: 1 fortified cultural landscape
- Nature: old-growth forest / glacial lakes / southern floodplain
- Cities: Grodno / Minsk / Polotsk
- Architecture/history: Nesvizh / Brest Fortress
- no chalk-quarry or industrial “blue lake” scene because access/safety and visual authenticity are too unstable
- no scene is selected simply because it is famous

## Hero

### H — Mir Castle / pond reflection

Working coordinate: **53.45108, 26.47272**  
Planned filename: `hero-mir-castle-reflection.webp`  

Scene identity:
- real Mir Castle Complex viewed across the landscaped pond
- red-brick defensive towers and pale residential wing clearly identifiable
- water reflection used as a major compositional element
- castle around the right half / right third with quieter sky and water available for Hero copy
- late spring or early autumn
- restrained warm morning or late-afternoon light
- architecture must preserve the actual Gothic / Renaissance / Baroque-derived massing rather than become a fantasy castle
- photo 60 / quiet watercolor 40
- no text, map, UI, border, collage, flag or invented skyline

Why this Hero:
- it is one of the most immediately recognisable built landscapes in Belarus
- the pond and park let the Hero read as a landscape, not a monument cut-out
- UNESCO describes the castle as a visible record of successive Gothic, Renaissance and Baroque influences
- the horizontal water foreground is robust for desktop and mobile crop

## Signature Facts

Exactly 3:

1. **世界遺産のストルーヴェ測地弧ポイント — 5地点**
   - UNESCO’s inscribed Struve Geodetic Arc contains 34 original station points across 10 countries; five are in Belarus.
   - This is unusually relevant to JOURNEY ATLAS because the sites are physical remnants of 19th-century measurement of the Earth.

2. **ベレジンスキーの未排水泥炭地 — 約11,000 ha**
   - UNESCO’s Berezinsky Biosphere Reserve describes one of Eastern Europe’s largest undrained peat bogs at about 11,000 ha.
   - Use this to show that Belarusian wetlands are a major geographic system, not empty flat land.

3. **ビャウォヴィエジャの森・世界遺産全体 — 141,885 ha**
   - UNESCO records the transboundary World Heritage property at 141,885 ha.
   - It includes extensive old-growth lowland forest and the property’s largest free-roaming European-bison population.
   - Label clearly that the area figure is for the Belarus–Poland property as a whole.

Do not replace these with a generic highest-point, forest-share or World-Heritage-count fact.

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **麦わらは、生活素材から立体の工芸へ変わる**
   - Belarusian straw weaving includes baskets, headgear, toys, accessories, harvest wreaths and geometric household ornaments.
   - UNESCO Representative List, 2022.

2. **一枚の紙を折り、左右対称の模様を切り出す**
   - Vytsinanka is the Belarusian paper-cutting tradition and remains a living craft rather than only museum decoration.
   - UNESCO Representative List, 2024.

3. **森では、木の洞や丸太の巣箱で蜂を育てる文化が残る**
   - Tree beekeeping recreates wild-bee conditions in tree / log hives and is shared with Poland.
   - UNESCO Representative List, 2020.

4. **南東部には、織りと刺繍を家族で継ぐ地域文化がある**
   - The Negliubka textile tradition uses woven towels, clothing, geometric and floral patterns.
   - UNESCO Urgent Safeguarding List, 2025.

5. **ブズラウでは、聖像をめぐる巡礼と祭りが共同体をつなぐ**
   - Budslaŭ fest combines pilgrimage, religious practice and gathering around the icon of Our Lady.
   - UNESCO Representative List, 2018.

6. **ミンスクでは、旧市街の断片と20世紀の再建都市が隣り合う**
   - Use the Svislach / Trinity Suburb to connect older urban fragments with the broader post-war city.
   - Final wording and source key must be verified during JSON implementation.

Keep these distinct from Taste, Signature Facts and the Scene descriptions.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月** — snowmelt and spring greening; wetlands and river floodplains can show higher water conditions.
2. **6〜8月** — the warmest period for lake and forest landscapes; city days can also be warm and humid.
3. **9〜11月** — forest and park colour changes; early autumn suits the visual language of Mir / Nesvizh / Białowieża.
4. **12〜2月** — cold continental winter with snow possible across much of the country; current safety guidance still overrides seasonal travel planning.
