## Page concept

**CAMBODIA / カンボジア**

Lead direction:

アンコールの石造寺院から、プノンペンの王宮、季節で姿を変えるトンレサップ湖、北部の断崖寺院、南西部の熱帯雨林、タイ湾の島、メコン川、カンポットの農地へ。クメール文明だけで終わらず、水と低地、森と海、現在の暮らしまでを一つの国の地理として見せる。

The page should reveal Cambodia through:
- Angkor Wat as the Hero because its five-tower silhouette is the clearest Cambodia-specific visual marker for a global audience
- one additional Angkor scene that feels physically different from the Hero rather than repeating the same monument view
- Phnom Penh as the contemporary capital and royal / river city
- Tonlé Sap as a seasonal hydrological system tied directly to settlement and livelihoods
- Preah Vihear as Khmer architecture adapted to a northern escarpment landscape
- the Cardamom region as Cambodia's humid forest layer
- the Gulf of Thailand coast / islands as a quieter maritime layer
- the Mekong corridor as a second major water geography distinct from Tonlé Sap
- Kampot countryside as cultivated landscape and food culture rather than another temple

- it is the strongest immediate visual identifier of Cambodia
- the long temple silhouette, palms and reflection work naturally in a horizontal Hero crop
- the quieter post-sunrise treatment keeps the page within the JOURNEY ATLAS visual language rather than a travel-poster aesthetic

1. **TONLÉ SAP / 水 — 川の流れが季節で反転する**

2. **COAST / 海 — 海岸線は約440〜443 km**

## Seasons / travel timing direction

Use three practical periods, with final weather claims rechecked before implementation:
- **11〜1月** — 比較的乾燥し、暑さがやや和らぐ。寺院や都市を歩きやすい時期。
- **2〜4月** — 乾季後半で暑さが強まる。日中の屋外移動は負荷が高い。
- **5〜10月** — 雨季。緑が濃くなり、トンレサップの水位が上がる一方、強い雨や道路状況を見込む必要がある。

Avoid claiming one universally “best” month.
