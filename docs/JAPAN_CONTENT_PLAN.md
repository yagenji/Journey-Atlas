# JAPAN — content plan

Updated: 2026-09-10  
Branch: `country/japan`

This document stores editorial and visual-design intent for JOURNEY ATLAS Japan. It does not connect unapproved Hero, Scene, Taste, or Map assets to production and does not change `atlasPublished:false`.

## Page concept

**JAPAN / 日本**

Lead direction:

雪を残す富士山と湖から、東京の交差点、京都の木造寺院、豪雪地の合掌造り、知床の森と湖、瀬戸内海の社、阿蘇の草原、屋久島の湿潤な森、八重山の海へ。都市・山地・火山・森林・海が短い距離で切り替わり、地域ごとの暮らしと食文化が重なる島国として見せる。

The page should reveal Japan through:
- Mount Fuji from Lake Kawaguchi as the Hero: instantly recognisable nationally, but geographically plausible and quiet rather than poster-like
- Tokyo as contemporary urban density
- Kyoto as wooden religious architecture embedded in a living city
- Shirakawa-go as snow-country settlement and vernacular architecture
- Shiretoko as northern lake / forest / mountain wilderness
- Miyajima as architecture shaped by tide and inland-sea geography
- Aso as volcanic grassland and caldera landscape
- Yakushima as wet cedar forest
- Ishigaki as subtropical island sea

The eight Scenes are not a ranking. They distribute climate, terrain, city/nature, built heritage, agriculture/settlement and island geography across the archipelago.

## Hero

### H — Mount Fuji / Lake Kawaguchi from Oishi Park

Working coordinate: **35.5233, 138.7465**  
Planned filename: `hero-fuji-lake-kawaguchi-oishi.webp`  
Content ID: `fuji-lake-kawaguchi-oishi`

Visual identity:
- real Mount Fuji viewed from the north shore of Lake Kawaguchi at / near Oishi Park
- the symmetrical volcanic cone is the unmistakable anchor; snow remains naturally on the upper slopes
- Lake Kawaguchi forms a broad horizontal foreground / middle ground and keeps the mountain grounded in a real viewpoint
- restrained lakeside vegetation or a narrow strip of seasonal flowers may appear only at the lower edge; do not turn the foreground into a floral poster
- clear spring morning or similarly soft daylight with natural visibility; restrained blue, green, stone and snow tones
- wide horizontal composition; Fuji remains legible in mobile crop while leaving calm negative space for Hero copy
- no Chureito Pagoda, torii, Kyoto roofline, Tokyo skyline or any landmark that is not geographically present at this viewpoint
- no giant sun, lenticular-cloud spectacle, mirror-perfect fantasy reflection, heavy saturation or tourism-poster treatment
- no crowds, tour buses, readable signs, flags, maps or UI
- photo 6 : quiet watercolor 4; refined illustrated realism consistent with Iceland / Norway
- one continuous scene only; no collage, panels, inset images or text

Why this Hero:
- Fuji is one of the few landscapes that can be recognised internationally as Japan without relying on text or a city skyline
- the Kawaguchiko viewpoint connects the national icon to real lake geography instead of assembling several Japanese symbols in one synthetic scene
- Tokyo, Kyoto and other cultural landscapes remain available for the eight Scenes, avoiding duplication in the Hero

Reference direction verified against JNTO Lake Kawaguchi guidance: the northern shore / Oishi Park is a real viewpoint toward Fuji and the lake.

## Locked 8-scene selection

### S01 — Tokyo / Shibuya Crossing

Working coordinate: **35.6595, 139.7005**  
Planned filename: `tokyo-shibuya-crossing.webp`  
Content ID: `tokyo-shibuya-crossing`

Scene identity:
- real Shibuya scramble crossing viewed from a modest elevated or street-corner perspective
- crossing stripes, compact city blocks, pedestrians and rail-station-edge urban density should read clearly

Why:
Tokyo adds a present-day metropolitan layer that is structurally different from the Hero and from the historic built landscapes.

### S02 — Kyoto / Kiyomizudera from the Okunoin side

### S03 — Shirakawa-go / Ogimachi from Shiroyama in winter

Shirakawa-go introduces the heavy-snow climate of inland central Japan and shows vernacular architecture as a response to geography and winter.

### S04 — Hokkaido / Shiretoko Five Lakes

Working coordinate: **44.125, 145.080**

Shiretoko gives the page a distinctly northern ecosystem of lakes, forest and volcanic mountains that contrasts with subtropical Ishigaki and wet Yakushima.

Miyajima adds a built landscape whose visual identity changes with the tide, connecting religious architecture directly to Japan's inland-sea geography.

### S06 — Kyushu / Aso / Kusasenri and Nakadake

### S07 — Yakushima / Shiratani Unsuikyo

Working coordinate: **30.3806, 130.5750**  
Planned filename: `yakushima-shiratani-unsuikyo.webp`  
Content ID: `yakushima-shiratani-unsuikyo`

Scene identity:
- real wet ravine forest with ancient cedar trunks, moss-covered stones and roots, filtered humid daylight and a small natural stream / wet rock where plausible
- dense layered greens but not neon; depth should come from real forest structure rather than fog effects
- no anime styling, fantasy spirit glow, oversized ancient tree, character reference or cinematic magical haze

Why:
Yakushima shows the warm, very wet forest end of Japan's climatic range and creates a strong contrast with Hokkaido, Aso and the urban scenes.

### S08 — Okinawa / Ishigaki / Kabira Bay

Working coordinate: **24.4514, 124.1444**  
Planned filename: `ishigaki-kabira-bay.webp`  
Content ID: `ishigaki-kabira-bay`

Scene identity:
- real Kabira Bay from the Kabira Park observation area
- emerald / turquoise water, white sand, dense subtropical shoreline and several small wooded islets visible in one continuous bay composition
- water color remains natural and restrained, with shallow/deeper tonal variation
- no swimmers, resort furniture, cocktails, giant boats or generic Maldives treatment
- glass-bottom boats only if tiny and geographically natural; landscape remains primary

Why:
Kabira Bay completes the archipelago with a subtropical island-sea landscape that cannot be inferred from Tokyo, Kyoto or Fuji alone.

## Signature Facts

Exactly 3:

1. **活火山 — 111**
   - Japan Meteorological Agency states Japan has 111 active volcanoes.
   - This explains why Fuji, Aso, hot springs and volcanic topography recur across very different regions without making them interchangeable.

2. **森林 — 国土の約3分の2**
   - Forestry Agency states forests cover two-thirds of Japan's land.
   - This corrects the city-heavy external image of Japan and supports the visual weight given to Shiretoko and Yakushima.

3. **世界遺産 — 27件**
   - UNESCO current Japan State Party page lists 27 properties: 22 cultural and 5 natural, including Ancient Capitals of Asuka and Fujiwara added in 2026.
   - This captures the breadth of cultural and natural heritage without reusing the Profile area / population figures.

Do not use airport codes, exchange-rate trivia, duplicated currency facts or generic highest-point filler.

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6 working topics; final wording must be source-checked during implementation:

1. **神社と寺** — 宗教施設は都市や集落の日常動線の近くにもあり、年中行事や参拝と重なる。
2. **共同浴場** — 温泉や銭湯では、湯船の外で体を洗ってから入浴する作法が空間の使い方にも表れる。
3. **駅を中心にした街** — 大都市では鉄道駅と商業・徒歩動線が強く結びつき、街の見え方をつくる。
4. **季節を食べる** — 旬の食材や季節の献立が、同じ料理でも地域・時期による違いを生む。
5. **里山** — 集落、農地、草地、森林が隣り合う、人の管理と自然が重なる景観が各地に残る。
6. **地域差** — 祭り、工芸、食、方言、住まいは全国一様ではなく、移動するほど違いが見える。

Avoid implying a single fixed “Japanese way of life.” Keep regional variation visible.

Content ID: `nigiri-sushi`

### FOOD04 — Japanese curry rice / カレーライス

Content ID: `japanese-curry-rice`  
Planned filename: `food-japanese-curry-rice.webp`

Dish identity:
- one simple plate only

- a few small cooked vegetables / meat pieces may be visible within the curry; no separate pickles or garnish dish
- no spoon, water glass, condiment, napkin or restaurant background
- centered and large, restrained matte food styling rather than commercial gloss

Why:
Curry rice adds an everyday home / casual-meal layer and gives the four dishes stronger format diversity than using two noodle dishes.

## Travel Scale

Shared three-step UI; all labels in days and final duration open-ended:

1. **5日** — 一つの都市圏と近郊を深く見る
   - 東京圏または京都・大阪圏など、一つの地域に絞り、街と近郊の景観を組み合わせる。
2. **9日** — 東西の二つの地域をつなぐ
   - 本州の主要都市軸を使い、都市景観に山間部・古都・瀬戸内など一つ異なる環境を加える。
3. **14日以上** — 列島の気候差まで広げる
   - 本州の軸に北海道、九州、沖縄など離れた地域を一つ加え、景観と暮らしの違いを比較できる日程にする。

Avoid long lists of example cities in visible copy. The point is travel scale, not itinerary ranking.

## Travel Notes

Exactly 3:

1. **列島は南北に長い** — 東京・京都だけの距離感で北海道や沖縄まで組み込むと移動時間が大きくなる。
2. **地方では交通密度が変わる** — 大都市の鉄道頻度を基準にせず、山間部・国立公園・離島は事前に接続を確認する。
