# ROMANIA — content plan

Updated: 2026-09-09  
Branch: `country/romania`

This document stores editorial and visual-design intent for JOURNEY ATLAS Romania. It does not connect unapproved visual assets to production and does not change `atlasPublished:false`.

## Page concept

**ROMANIA / ルーマニア**

Lead direction:

カルパチアの森に建つ王宮から、トランシルヴァニアの中世都市、山岳道路、ブコヴィナの壁画教会、マラムレシュの木造文化、地下の岩塩坑、そして黒海へ開くドナウ・デルタまで。山地と川、複数の歴史圏、農村の暮らしが重なってできた国をたどる。

The page should reveal Romania through:
- a Carpathian royal landscape rather than reducing the country to Dracula imagery
- Transylvania as a lived geographic and urban region, not a fantasy backdrop
- a strong mountain-road experience through the Făgăraș range
- the Danube Delta as a vast wetland world at the Black Sea edge
- painted Orthodox churches in Bucovina and timber architecture in Maramureș
- underground industrial landscape at Turda
- the unusual mud-volcano geology of Buzău Land
- Bucharest as the practical capital and modern-history layer even though it is not forced into the scenic eight when stronger visual discoveries exist elsewhere

- late-afternoon natural light or clear soft daylight
- restrained green, stone, timber and mountain-blue palette

Historical anchor:

### S04 — Danube Delta / Mila 23 and the Old Danube channels

Why it is included:
The Danube reaches the Black Sea by becoming a maze of water, reed and lakes. UNESCO lists the Romanian Danube Delta property at 312,440 ha and notes more than 300 bird species, making this a geographic world unlike the country's mountain interior.

### S05 — Voroneț / Church of St George

### S08 — Buzău Land / Pâclele Mari Mud Volcanoes

Working coordinate: 45.3581, 26.7124  
Planned filename: `buzau-paclele-mari-mud-volcanoes.webp`  
Content ID: `buzau-paclele-mari-mud-volcanoes`

Scene identity:
- real Pâclele Mari mud-volcano field
- low grey-brown cones, cracked mud surface and sparse salt-tolerant vegetation
- surrounding Subcarpathian hills visible enough to prevent a generic desert reading
- active bubbling only at believable scale
- no lava, flames or true volcanic eruption imagery
- neutral daylight; strange geology should carry the scene without fantasy color

Why it is included:
UNESCO's Buzău Land Global Geopark explains that these are gas-driven mud features rather than magmatic volcanoes. The scene gives Romania an unexpected geological layer far removed from the familiar castle-and-forest image.

## Travel Scale

### 3〜4日 / city
**ブカレストからシナイアとブラショフへ**

首都を入口に、鉄道や車でカルパチアの谷へ移動し、PeleșとBrașovまでをつなぐ。短い滞在では都市数を増やしすぎず、首都・王宮・山岳都市という三つの違いをつかむ。  
例：ブカレスト → シナイア → ブラショフ → ブカレスト。

### 7〜10日 / map
**トランシルヴァニアを横断し、山と地下へ入る**

Bucharest / SinaiaからBrașov、Sighișoara、Sibiu方面、Turda / Clujまでをつなぐ。Transfăgărășanを組み込む場合は道路状況と季節を優先し、閉鎖時は一般道・鉄道中心のルートへ切り替える。  
例：ブカレスト → シナイア → ブラショフ → シギショアラ → シビウ → トゥルダ／クルジュ。

### 14日以上 / compass
**北の木造文化とブコヴィナ、最後にドナウ・デルタまで広げる**

TransylvaniaにMaramureșとBucovinaを加え、さらに時間を取れるならTulceaからDanube Deltaへ。山越えと地方移動が多いため、全8景を詰め込むより地域ごとに2泊以上の余白を置く。  
例：ブカレスト → トランシルヴァニア → マラムレシュ → ブコヴィナ → ブカレスト／トゥルチャ → ドナウ・デルタ。

The final bucket remains open-ended. Do not imply that a full north-to-delta circuit is comfortable in exactly 14 days for every traveler.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - cities, Saxon towns and lower rural landscapes return to walking season
   - Mărțișor gives early March a clear cultural marker
   - high mountain roads / trails can remain winter-affected; never infer Transfăgărășan access from lowland spring weather

2. **6〜8月**
   - strongest window for high mountains, long rural driving days and Danube Delta boat travel
   - Transfăgărășan access must still be checked from current road information, not assumed from the month alone
   - major mountain / castle destinations are busiest in midsummer

3. **9〜10月**
   - strong direction for Carpathian forest color, Transylvanian towns and rural landscapes
   - daylight shortens but driving / walking conditions can remain comfortable before winter weather
   - avoid promising fixed autumn-color dates

4. **11〜2月**
   - travel emphasis shifts toward Bucharest, towns, museums, castles and winter mountain bases
   - high road access is restricted and rural transfers need more buffer
   - snow is likely in mountain areas but should never be promised for lowland / city scenes

## Primary Themes

Editorial recommendation:

- **history / 時をたどる**
- **earth / 地球の風景**
- **life / 暮らしに出会う**

Rationale:
- history is a destination-level motive across royal Romania, Transylvanian Saxon cities, Bucovina, Dacian / Roman sites and 20th-century Bucharest / Brâncuși
- earth is a strong reason to choose Romania because the Carpathians, Făgăraș road landscape, Danube Delta and Buzău geology are not secondary scenery
- life is strong enough to qualify through Maramureș, pastoral landscapes, craft traditions, Mărțișor, ceramics and village architecture

Do not assign `road` solely because Transfăgărășan is iconic. The road scene is important, but the broader country-selection motives are stronger under history / earth / life.

Theme assignment remains exclusively in `data/theme-taxonomy.json` and is implemented only with the final Country package; do not duplicate theme labels in Country JSON.
