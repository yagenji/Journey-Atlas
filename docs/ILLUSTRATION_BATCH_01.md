## Batch QA

The batch should not be approved as a group until all of the following are true:

- no image reads as literal photography;
- no image drifts into watercolor / cartoon treatment;
- every scene is a real named place or a verified real landscape;
- architecture and geography remain plausible;
- neighboring cards do not repeat the same composition or dominant palette;
- at least four clearly different visual families appear across the batch: mountain/ice, desert/rock, architecture/city, tropical/sea;
- all images survive a 16:10 destination-card crop;
- no text, flags, logos, labels, frames or UI appear inside the art.

## PM decision rule

Routine scene refinement, crop adjustment and regeneration proceed without asking the user. Escalate only if the chosen real scene itself needs to change in a way that materially alters the visual identity or editorial meaning of the destination.
