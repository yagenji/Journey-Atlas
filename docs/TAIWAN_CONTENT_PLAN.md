## Page concept

**TAIWAN / 台湾**

Lead direction:

都市の密度から少し離れると、島の中央に高山が連なり、東海岸では山地が太平洋まで迫る。中央の湖、阿里山の森、南部の熱帯海岸、澎湖の玄武岩へ進むほど、夜市・寺廟・茶・鉄道と地形の違いが重なって見えてくる島。

The page should reveal Taiwan through:
- a real Taipei night-market streetscape as the Hero, using food stalls, scooters, temple architecture and evening street life as immediately recognisable Taiwan cues
- Taipei 101 and the dense modern city embedded in green hills as a separate Scene
- Jiufen as a historic hillside settlement tied to the northern coast
- Sun Moon Lake as the central mountain-lake landscape
- Alishan as forest, railway and high-elevation cultural landscape
- Hehuanshan as the high-mountain spine
- Tainan as the long urban-history layer
- Kenting as the tropical southern coast
- Penghu as offshore basalt-island geology
- night markets, tea, temples, indigenous cultures and rail travel as lived layers rather than tourist checklists

## Travel Scale

### 3〜4日 / city
**台北と北部の丘陵を往復する**

台北の街・夜市・寺廟を軸に、鉄道やバスで九份や北部の山沿いへ出る。短い旅では島を一周しようとせず、都市と山地の距離感をつかむ。
例：台北 → 九份 → 台北。

### 7〜10日 / map
**西部の都市軸から中央の山へ入る**

高速鉄道・鉄道を背骨に、台北から台中／日月潭、嘉義／阿里山、台南へ南下する。山岳部は平地の都市間移動とは別に時間を取る。
例：台北 → 日月潭 → 阿里山 → 台南 → 高雄 → 台北。

### 14日以上 / compass
**西部縦断に東海岸か離島を一つ加える**

東海岸の花蓮・台東、または澎湖を別の地域ブロックとして加える。山岳道路・天候・フェリー／国内線条件に余白を置き、全地点を詰め込まない。
例：台北 → 花蓮 → 台東 → 高雄 → 台南 → 阿里山 → 台北。

The final bucket stays open-ended. A complete main-island circuit plus Penghu should not be implied as effortless within a fixed upper limit.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - lower elevations are generally comfortable for city walking and regional travel
   - rainfall and humidity increase toward the plum-rain period
   - mountain conditions can still differ sharply from lowland temperatures

2. **6〜9月**
   - hot and humid in the lowlands
   - typhoons, heavy rain, landslides and transport disruption can change mountain / east-coast plans
   - keep weather-sensitive routes flexible rather than promising fixed access

3. **10〜11月**
   - often a useful period for cities, coast and mountain travel with lower heat
   - northeast monsoon conditions can make the north and east wetter / windier
   - high-elevation temperature drops remain significant

4. **12〜2月**
   - mild to cool in many lowland areas
   - the north and east can be damp while southern Taiwan is often milder
   - high mountains may experience freezing conditions and occasional snow; do not describe Taiwan as uniformly tropical
