## Page concept

**INDONESIA / インドネシア**

Lead direction:

ボロブドゥールの石のストゥーパから、火山のカルデラ、バリの水田、乾いた島の稜線、石灰岩の多島海、スラウェシの木造家屋、スマトラの巨大カルデラ湖、ジャカルタの都市景観、カリマンタンの熱帯雨林へ。海によって分かれた島々を移動するほど、地形、宗教、建築、食、暮らしが大きく切り替わる国。

The page should reveal Indonesia through:
- Borobudur as the Hero because its perforated bell-shaped stupas are one of the most immediately recognisable Indonesia-specific cultural landscapes and create a calm horizontal entrance
- Bromo-Tengger-Semeru as the volcanic layer that explains the archipelago's active geology
- Bali's subak rice terraces as a living agricultural landscape rather than a resort image
- Padar / Komodo National Park as the dry-island savanna and marine-island layer of eastern Indonesia
- Raja Ampat as a radically different humid karst-island seascape in the far east
- Toraja tongkonan as a distinct Sulawesi architectural and social landscape
- Lake Toba as the scale of Sumatra's volcanic geography
- Jakarta as the contemporary metropolitan layer
- Tanjung Puting as the lowland rainforest / river layer of Kalimantan

The Hero is not repeated inside the eight Scenes.

## Hero

### H — Borobudur / upper circular terraces at dawn

Working coordinate: **-7.6079, 110.2038**  
Planned filename: `hero-borobudur-upper-terraces.webp`  
Content ID: `borobudur-upper-terraces-hero`

Visual identity:
- real Borobudur Temple in Central Java, seen from the upper circular terraces
- multiple unmistakable perforated bell-shaped stupas visible in foreground and midground, with the monumental central stupa clearly anchoring the temple identity
- broad horizontal 3:2 composition; one continuous camera view from terrace level, not an aerial map-like view
- distant Kedu Valley and volcanic silhouette may appear softly through natural morning haze, but the stone stupas remain the dominant subject
- soft early-morning light with restrained warm-grey volcanic stone, muted green valley and pale sky; no saturated orange tourism-poster sunrise
- photo 6 : quiet watercolor 4; refined painterly realism, visibly illustrated on close inspection
- preserve real proportions and stone texture; no invented giant Buddha, fantasy temple extensions, prayer flags, ceremonial crowd, readable signage, labels or text
- leave enough quiet atmospheric space that Hero copy does not fight the landmark while ensuring the stupas survive mobile crop

Why this Hero:
- UNESCO identifies Borobudur as an 8th–9th century Buddhist monument with three circular platforms surrounded by 72 openwork stupas, giving it a highly specific silhouette
- the view is culturally specific to Indonesia and visually distinct from the natural-landscape Scenes that follow
- the repeated stupa forms create depth without needing spectacle or heavy saturation

## Locked 8-scene selection

### S01 — Bromo Tengger Semeru / Penanjakan view

Working coordinate: **-7.9080, 112.9510**  
Planned filename: `bromo-penanjakan-caldera.webp`  
Content ID: `bromo-penanjakan-caldera`

Scene identity:
- real classic view from the Penanjakan area across the Tengger caldera
- Mount Bromo's smoking crater cone and Mount Batok clearly separated in the foreground/midground, with Semeru rising farther behind when visibility allows

## Coverage check

Hero + 8 Scenes distribute:
- Central Java — Borobudur Hero / Buddhist monumental landscape
- East Java — Bromo / active volcanic caldera
- Bali — subak rice agriculture
- East Nusa Tenggara — Padar / dry island + sea
- Southwest Papua — Raja Ampat / humid karst archipelago
- South Sulawesi — Toraja / vernacular architecture + highlands
- North Sumatra — Lake Toba / giant volcanic lake
- Jakarta — contemporary metropolis
- Central Kalimantan — rainforest river

Visual types:
- stone stupas + misty valley
- volcanic cones + sand sea
- rice terraces + irrigation
- dry ridges + crescent bays
- forested karst islets + tropical sea
- curved-roof timber houses + rice fields
- caldera lake + highland ridges
- fountain monument + dense skyline
- rainforest river corridor

No Scene repeats the Borobudur Hero. Padar and Raja Ampat are intentionally separated because the first is a dry savanna-island panorama and the second a humid limestone-islet seascape. Bromo and Lake Toba are intentionally separated because Bromo is an active cone-and-sand-sea volcanic landscape while Toba is a vast lake-filled caldera system.

## Travel scale direction

Keep all durations in days.

- **5日** — one region only, e.g. Bali or Central Java; do not imply the archipelago can be sampled meaningfully in a short trip
- **10日** — combine two nearby regions, e.g. Central/East Java + Bali, with one substantial land/rail/flight transition
- **14日以上** — add a remote island region such as Komodo / Sulawesi / Raja Ampat; domestic flights and boat weather become part of itinerary design

Avoid cramming three or four far-separated island groups into the middle band merely to make the itinerary look comprehensive.

## Transport direction

Use the standard Japanese transport-card titles and shared icon system.

Core logic:
- long inter-island movement — domestic air is often the practical backbone
- Java / some urban corridors — rail is useful where the network exists
- island / national-park access — ferries and boats matter, but schedules and weather can shape the trip
- local movement — car + driver, taxi / ride-hailing, buses or scooters vary strongly by region

Keep transport copy practical and region-specific; do not imply one mode works equally across the whole country.
