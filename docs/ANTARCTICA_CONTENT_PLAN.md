# ANTARCTICA — content plan

Updated: 2026-08-23

This is the next-country production brief for JOURNEY ATLAS. Source material is limited to places/scenes already published in JOURNEY LENS plus authoritative place coordinates for map placement.

## Page concept

**ANTARCTICA / 南極**

Lead direction: Drake Passageの先で、雪、氷、海、野生動物が同じ風景の中に現れる。静けさと巨大さを、白一色ではなく青・岩・海・動物の色まで含めて見せる。

Hero: **Lemaire Channel**. Use the real channel as a single coherent landscape. The illustration should be luminous and spacious rather than monochrome/dark; blue-white ice, pale sky and dark rock should create depth.

1. **Hydrurga Rocks / Chinstrap Penguins**  
   Journey Lens source: first Antarctic landing; chinstrap penguins moving between sea and nests.  
   Map reference: -64.12, -61.33 (Journey Lens story coordinate).

2. **Portal Point / Continental Landing**  
   First step onto the Antarctic continent; iceberg-filled bay and distant whales.  
   Map reference: -64.50000, -61.76667.

3. **Flandres Bay / Humpback Whales**  
   Snow and mist over quiet water; humpbacks surfacing and bubble-net feeding.  
   Map reference: -65.05, -63.09 (Journey Lens story coordinate).

4. **Lemaire Channel**  
   Narrow ice-filled channel with glaciers and cliffs pressing in from both sides.  
   Map reference: -65.06667, -63.95000.

5. **Orcas beside the ship**  
   A pod appearing after the route changed because of ice and weather. Use the Lemaire/Booth Island area as the story context; final pin should be refined from the voyage log before release.

6. **Neumayer Channel**  
   Clearer evening light after a day of changing plans; mountains and ice become sharply visible.  
   Map reference: -64.78333, -63.45000.

7. **Graham Passage / Emperor Penguin**  
   A single young emperor penguin standing on a large iceberg.  
   Map reference: -64.40000, -61.51667.

8. **Palaver Point / Chinstrap Colony**  
   Final landing, noisy rookery, snow slope and broad view from higher ground.  
   Map reference: -64.15000, -61.76667.

## Visual direction

- Locked JOURNEY ATLAS style: approximately **photo 60 / illustration 40**.
- Every image must be based on a real named Antarctic place or a real voyage encounter tied to a verified location.
- Keep enough painterly simplification that the images do not read as stock photography.
- Do not use watercolor wash / gouache-poster treatment as the primary look.
- Avoid making every scene blue-grey; introduce pale turquoise water, dark rock, snow white, soft sky blue and restrained warm accents from wildlife/equipment.
- Wildlife should be part of the landscape, not oversized character illustrations.
- No fictional icebergs, mountains or impossible wildlife groupings added only for drama.
- Hero and scene art should look inviting while preserving the specific Antarctic geography.
- Map should be an illustrated peninsula/cruise-area map rather than a full-continent scientific map, because the actual journey is concentrated around the Antarctic Peninsula.
