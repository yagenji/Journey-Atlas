## 8 SCENES
1. Glenveagh National Park / Lough Veagh and Glenveagh Castle
   - Region: County Donegal / northwest.
   - Coordinate: approx. 55.053, -7.949.
   - View: across Lough Veagh toward the castle and surrounding Derryveagh Mountains.
   - Season: early autumn.
   - Composition: long lake foreground, castle small but identifiable, low mountain enclosure.
   - Purpose: remote northwest, glacial valley, estate landscape.

2. Diamond Hill / Connemara National Park
   - Region: County Galway / west.
   - Coordinate: approx. 53.548, -9.914.
   - View: elevated trail / upper slopes toward the Twelve Bens, blanket bog and Atlantic in the distance.
   - Season: late spring.
   - Composition: broad layered mountain-and-bog panorama; no alpine exaggeration.
   - Purpose: western bog, quartzite mountain, Atlantic-facing uplands.

3. The Burren / Mullaghmore and limestone pavement
   - Region: County Clare / west.
   - Coordinate: approx. 53.006, -9.030.
   - View: low-to-mid elevation across pale limestone pavement toward Mullaghmore.
   - Season: late spring, when small wildflowers can appear in fissures.
   - Composition: limestone foreground texture, rounded bare hill, restrained green patches.
   - Purpose: karst geology unlike the greener Irish stereotype.

4. Killarney National Park / Ladies View
   - Region: County Kerry / southwest.
   - Coordinate: approx. 51.983, -9.594.
   - View: established roadside viewpoint across the Upper Lake / Middle Lake system toward MacGillycuddy's Reeks.
   - Season: early summer.
   - Composition: layered lakes, wooded slopes, mountains receding through moist air.
   - Purpose: lake-and-mountain landscape of southwest Ireland.

5. Sceilg Mhichíl / Skellig Michael
   - Region: County Kerry / Atlantic offshore.
   - Coordinate: 51.77194, -10.53861.
   - View: from permitted landing / approach perspective showing the steep rock island and recognizable stone monastic beehive cells, without impossible aerial dramatization.
   - Season: summer visitor season.
   - Composition: steep rock mass, Atlantic sea, monastic structures clearly secondary but identifiable.
   - Purpose: Atlantic isolation + early Christian monastic heritage; UNESCO property.

6. Rock of Cashel
   - Region: County Tipperary / south-central.
   - Coordinate: approx. 52.520, -7.890.
   - View: from lower fields / town approach, showing the limestone outcrop and clustered medieval ecclesiastical buildings.
   - Season: early autumn.
   - Composition: horizontal green foreground, elevated stone complex against soft sky.
   - Purpose: medieval religious and royal history in an inland agricultural landscape.

7. Glendalough / Monastic City and Upper Valley
   - Region: County Wicklow / east.
   - Coordinate: Round Tower reference 53.01097, -6.32767.
   - View: ground-level within/near the monastic site with round tower identifiable and wooded glacial valley behind.
   - Season: late autumn.
   - Composition: stone ruins and round tower integrated with valley; not fantasy reconstruction.
   - Purpose: early Christian monastic history + Wicklow valley landscape.

8. Brú na Bóinne / Newgrange
   - Region: County Meath / northeast.
   - Coordinate: approx. 53.6947, -6.4755.
   - View: outside the reconstructed kerbstone/entrance frontage, showing the circular passage tomb mound in its Boyne Valley setting.
   - Season: cool early spring.
   - Composition: low monumental mound, white quartz frontage recognizable but restrained; open agricultural landscape.
   - Purpose: prehistoric Ireland; UNESCO property.

## BEYOND THE SCENERY
1. CITY / Dublin
   - River Liffey, Georgian streets, compact central districts, literature and contemporary city life.
2. HISTORY / A long layered past
   - Passage tombs, early monasteries, Norman and Gaelic power, British rule, independence and partition presented carefully and factually.
3. LIFE / Irish language and Gaeltacht
   - Irish remains the first official language; western Gaeltacht areas reveal a living linguistic landscape rather than a museum piece.
4. FOOD / Bread, dairy, seafood
   - Soda/brown bread, dairy, Atlantic seafood and regional ingredients; avoid reducing Irish food culture to pub clichés.
5. ROAD / Wild Atlantic Way
   - The Atlantic coast becomes legible through a long road journey; travel pace matters more than ticking off stops.
6. WILDLIFE / Atlantic seabirds
   - Puffins and other seabirds are seasonally visible around offshore cliffs and islands; timing and protected-area rules matter.

## TRAVEL TRIVIA — PLANNED
1. TRANSPORT / 左側通行
   - Drive on the left; roundabouts and narrow rural roads require extra attention.
2. LANGUAGE / 二つの地名表記
   - Irish and English place names appear together in many areas; Gaeltacht signage can foreground Irish.
3. PRACTICAL / Type G plugs
   - Ireland uses 230V / Type G plugs; visitors from Japan need an adapter.
4. BOOKING / Newgrange
   - Access to Newgrange is controlled through Brú na Bóinne Visitor Centre and advance booking is important.
5. WEATHER / One day, several skies
   - Atlantic weather changes quickly; waterproof outer layers are more useful than relying on a single forecast.

## SEASONS
- 5–6月: long daylight, spring/early-summer vegetation, generally useful for walking and road travel.
- 7–8月: longest main visitor season; accommodation and popular sites require earlier planning.
- 9–10月: cooler, changing light and quieter routes; Atlantic weather becomes more variable.
- 11–4月: short days, wet/windy periods and reduced seasonal access to some island/coastal experiences; city and heritage travel remains possible.

## TRANSPORT
