## PHASE 1 — CONTENT DESIGN

### HERO
- Place: Vilnius Historic Centre from Subačius Panoramic Viewpoint
- Japanese: ヴィリニュス旧市街 / Subačius展望台
- Coordinate: 54.677366, 25.294216
- Viewpoint: Elevated panorama from the southeast/east side of the Old Town, looking across red roofs and church towers toward Gediminas Hill / Three Crosses area.
- Season: Late spring to early summer.
- Light: Clear late-afternoon to restrained golden-hour light.
- Composition: Wide horizontal panorama. Old Town roofs and church towers occupy the middle band, green slopes/trees provide a quiet foreground, sky remains open but not cloud-heavy. No modern high-rise dominance.
- Visual reason: Shows the layered Baroque/Gothic city, hills and greenery in one frame and creates a clear Lithuanian identity before moving into dunes, lakes, forests and archaeological landscapes.
- Hero lead: 「赤い屋根と教会塔が連なるヴィリニュスから、砂丘、湖、森、丘城へ。低く穏やかな地形の中に、バルトの歴史が重なる国。」

### 8 SCENES

1. Curonian Spit / Parnidis Dune
- Japanese: クルシュー砂州 / パルニディス砂丘
- Region: Klaipėda / Neringa, western Lithuania
- Coordinate: 55.29505, 20.99153
- Viewpoint: From the Parnidis Dune viewpoint/trail, looking across the pale dune toward the Curonian Lagoon and pine-covered spit.
- Season: Late summer.
- Light: Clear afternoon, blue sky, restrained warm light.
- Composition: Sweeping dune line diagonally across frame; lagoon visible beyond; pine forest as scale reference. Do not exaggerate into a desert or create cliffs.
- Description: 「バルト海とクルシュー潟湖の間に延びる細長い砂州。ニダ南側のパルニディス砂丘では、砂、松林、水面が細い陸地の中で隣り合う。」

2. Trakai Island Castle
- Japanese: トラカイ島城
- Region: Trakai / southeast
- Coordinate: 54.6525, 24.9331
- Viewpoint: Three-quarter view from the Galvė Lake shore / pedestrian approach, castle clearly isolated on the island.
- Season: Early autumn.
- Light: Soft morning or late-afternoon light; calm water.
- Composition: Red-brick Gothic castle centered slightly off-axis, lake in foreground, low wooded shoreline behind. No fantasy additions.
- Description: 「ガルヴェ湖の島に建つ赤レンガの城。中世のリトアニア大公国の政治拠点の一つで、湖と要塞建築が同じ景観に収まる。」

3. Hill of Crosses
- Japanese: 十字架の丘
- Region: Šiauliai / northern Lithuania
- Coordinate: 56.0153, 23.4160
- Viewpoint: Eye-level along the central path, showing dense layers of large and small crosses rather than a distant generic hill.
- Season: Late spring.
- Light: Clear but soft daylight; avoid dark dramatic storm treatment.
- Composition: Path leads inward between crosses; horizon low; craftsmanship and accumulated scale visible.
- Description: 「大小の十字架が幾重にも置かれ続ける北部の巡礼地。信仰だけでなく、帝政期やソ連期を通じて残った記憶と抵抗の象徴でもある。」

4. Kaunas Modernism / Central Post Office and Laisvės Avenue
- Japanese: カウナス・モダニズム
- Region: Kaunas / central Lithuania
- Coordinate: 54.897928, 23.904424
- Viewpoint: Street-level architectural view around the Central Post Office / Laisvės Avenue modernist ensemble.
- Season: Late spring.
- Light: Bright mid-morning, soft shadows.
- Composition: Human-scale boulevard and interwar façades; architecture remains primary; no generic contemporary skyline.
- Description: 「戦間期に臨時首都となったカウナスで育ったモダニズム建築群。短い期間に行政、文化、住宅の街並みが近代国家の姿として形づくられた。」

5. Aukštaitija National Park / Ladakalnis
- Japanese: アウクシュタイティヤ国立公園 / ラダカルニス
- Region: Northeast Lithuania
- Coordinate: 55.3664, 25.9908
- Viewpoint: Ladakalnis panorama over the surrounding lake district.
- Season: High summer.
- Light: Clear late afternoon.
- Composition: Layered blue lakes between pine and mixed forest, gentle moraine hills, broad horizon. No alpine mountains.
- Description: 「北東部の湖沼地帯を見渡すラダカルニス。氷河地形の低い丘と森の間に湖が連なり、平坦な国土の中にも細かな起伏があることが分かる。」

6. Kernavė Archaeological Site
- Japanese: ケルナヴェ考古遺跡
- Region: Širvintos / east-central Lithuania
- Coordinate: 54.8790, 24.8530
- Viewpoint: Elevated view across the green hillfort complex toward the Neris valley.
- Season: Early summer.
- Light: Soft evening light.
- Composition: Repeating grass-covered hillfort forms, valley and forest beyond; no reconstructed medieval city.
- Description: 「ネリス川沿いに複数の丘城と集落跡が残る考古景観。中世国家形成以前からの居住の痕跡が、緑の地形そのものとして見える。」

7. Čepkeliai Marsh
- Japanese: チェプケリャイ湿原
- Region: Dzūkija / southern Lithuania
- Coordinate: 54.0220, 24.4270
- Viewpoint: Official educational trail / observation area across bog pools, low pines and forested islands.
- Season: Early autumn.
- Light: Clear cool morning, slight mist acceptable.
- Composition: Wetland foreground, scattered water pools, low pine and distant forest islands; no dramatic mountains.
- Description: 「南部ズーキヤに広がる大きな高層湿原。水面、小さな島状の森、砂地の松林が連続し、人の立入りが厳しく管理される自然保護区でもある。」

8. Vilnius Old Town street / Užupis edge
- Japanese: ヴィリニュス旧市街 / ウジュピス
- Region: Vilnius
- Coordinate: 54.6816, 25.2950
- Viewpoint: Street-level transition around Užupis / Vilnelė, with historic façades, church architecture and green river edge.
- Season: Late spring.
- Light: Morning.
- Composition: Narrow historic street and river/greenery relationship, people small and secondary, no souvenir-poster treatment.
- Description: 「旧市街からヴィルネレ川を越えてウジュピスへ歩くと、歴史建築と小さな路地、緑の斜面が短い距離で切り替わる。首都を“歩く街”として見せる景観。」

## COPY DESIGN

### Country Profile
- Region: Northern Europe / Baltic region
- Capital: Vilnius
- Population: 約289万人（2026年8月初め、暫定値）
- Area: 約65,300 km²
- Language: Lithuanian
- Main religion: Roman Catholicism (74.2% in 2021 statistical survey)
- Currency: Euro (EUR)

### Signature Facts
1. UNESCO World Heritage properties — 5
   - Vilnius Historic Centre, Curonian Spit, Kernavė Archaeological Site, Struve Geodetic Arc, Modernist Kaunas.
2. National parks — 5
   - Aukštaitija, Dzūkija, Curonian Spit, Žemaitija and Trakai Historical National Park.
3. Forest cover — 33.91%
   - 2025 forest coverage, 2,214,323 ha.

### Beyond the Scenery
1. CITY / 街を歩く — 「ヴィリニュスは路地と丘でできている」
   - Old Town, Užupis, Vilnelė and viewpoints are close enough to connect on foot.
2. HISTORY / 時をたどる — 「カウナスで“命のビザ”の記憶をたどる」
   - 1939〜1940年、日本の外交官・杉原千畝はカウナスの日本副領事として勤務した。1940年7〜8月、ナチスの迫害やソ連支配から逃れようとした主にユダヤ系の難民へ日本通過ビザを発給し、多くの人々の脱出を助けた。
   - 日本外務省によれば現存するビザ発給リストは2,140名分で、家族単位の渡航や未記載分もあるため、実際に救われた人は数千人規模と考えられている。
   - 当時の日本領事館だった建物は現在「杉原記念館（Sugihara House）」として公開され、カウナスの戦間期と第二次世界大戦期をつなぐ場所になっている。
   - 1984年、杉原はヤド・ヴァシェムから「諸国民の中の正義の人」に認定された。
3. LIFE / 暮らしに出会う — 「歌が大人数の共同体になる」
   - Lithuanian Song and Dance Celebration is part of UNESCO-recognised Baltic song and dance celebrations.
4. CULTURE / 文化に触れる — 「十字架は建造物ではなく、続いている技術」
   - Cross-crafting includes making, consecration and rituals and is UNESCO intangible heritage.
5. FOOD / 食をめぐる — 「じゃがいも、黒パン、乳製品の北東ヨーロッパ」
   - Use cepelinai, rye bread, curd products and seasonal forest foods as supporting food story; avoid claiming a single national dish.
6. ROAD / 道の先へ — 「都市の間は公共交通、自然へは車を足す」
   - Vilnius–Kaunas–Klaipėda axis is practical by rail/bus; car becomes useful for dispersed parks and rural scenes; Curonian Spit requires ferry access from Klaipėda side.

### Travel Trivia
1. LANGUAGE / 言葉 — 「Ačiūは“ありがとう”」
   - Lithuanian is the state language; include simple phrases Labas / Ačiū / Prašau.
2. HISTORY / 歴史 — 「1940年、カウナスで発給された“命のビザ”」
   - 杉原千畝はカウナスの日本副領事として、1940年7〜8月に日本通過ビザを発給した。現在の杉原記念館は、当時の日本領事館として使われた建物にある。
3. TRADITION / 伝統 — 「十字架は今も増え続ける」
   - Hill of Crosses remains an active pilgrimage site where visitors leave crosses and rosaries.
4. FOREST / 森 — 「秋はキノコ採りが日常のレジャーになる」
   - Foraging for mushrooms and berries is a strong seasonal tradition, especially in Dzūkija.
5. MUSIC / 音 — 「Sutartinėsは北東部の多声歌」
   - UNESCO-listed multipart singing tradition associated with northeastern Lithuania.

### Seasons
- 6〜8月: Long days; best balance for Curonian Spit, lake districts, outdoor city walking and festivals.
- 9〜10月: Forest colour and mushroom season; quieter outside cities; mornings become colder.
- 11〜3月: Short days, freezing conditions and variable snow; strongest for urban architecture, museums and winter atmosphere.
- 4〜5月: Thaw, fresh green landscapes and returning birdlife; weather remains changeable.

### For Whom
1. 「建築と歴史を歩いてつなげたい人」
   - Vilnius Baroque, Trakai, Kernavė and Kaunas modernism create a compact historical route.
2. 「大きな山ではなく、湖・森・湿地の静かな景観が好きな人」
   - Aukštaitija, Dzūkija and Curonian Spit reveal low-relief Baltic nature.
3. 「バルト三国を陸路で比べたい人」
   - Lithuania changes the Baltic sequence through Catholic heritage, Grand Duchy history and a different balance of forest, city and farmland.

### Travel Notes
1. 「自然景観は標高より光と季節で変わる」
   - Much of the country is low-relief; morning/evening light and seasonal colour matter more than dramatic elevation.
2. 「Čepkeliaiは自由に歩ける一般公園ではない」
   - Access to the strict reserve and educational trail is controlled; current rules must be checked before visiting.
3. 「Curonian Spitは移動時間を余分に見る」
   - Ferry access and the long narrow peninsula mean short map distances can still require time.
