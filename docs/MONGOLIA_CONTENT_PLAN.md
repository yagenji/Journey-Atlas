# MONGOLIA — content plan

Updated: 2026-09-09  
Branch: `country/mongolia`

This document stores editorial and visual-design intent for JOURNEY ATLAS Mongolia. It does not connect unapproved visual assets to production and does not change `atlasPublished:false`.

## Page concept

**MONGOLIA / モンゴル**

Lead direction:

草原を走る馬と白いゲルの先に、ゴビの砂丘、北の森と湖、西の氷河、古都カラコルムが大きな距離を置いて続く。広い国土を移動するほど、遊牧の暮らしと地形の変化が地図の距離として見えてくる国。

The page should reveal Mongolia through:
- an immediately recognisable Gobi landscape as the Hero, without reducing the whole country to desert
- living pastoral landscapes in the Orkhon Valley
- northern taiga / alpine-lake geography at Khövsgöl
- glaciated high mountains in the Mongolian Altai
- red Gobi badlands distinct from the Hero's dune system
- Ulaanbaatar as a lived capital and Buddhist religious centre
- Kharkhorum / Erdene Zuu as the political and religious history layer
- Bronze Age deer stones as evidence that Mongolia's steppe history extends far earlier than the Mongol Empire
- takhi in Hustai as a wildlife-conservation landscape rather than a zoo-style animal portrait

Content ID: `khongoryn-els-gobi`

Visual identity:

- long amber sand ridge clearly separated from the darker Gobi mountain wall behind it
- narrow green / muted seasonal strip around the Khongoryn Gol side may appear where geographically plausible
- one or two Bactrian camels may appear at natural scale; a very small white ger may be present only as a secondary spatial cue
- no posed camel caravan, no costume portrait, no oversized camp
- broad horizontal composition; stronger dune mass on the right / centre and quieter sky / open terrain available for Hero copy
- late-afternoon or clear soft daylight, restrained warm sand against cool mountain tones
- real dune and mountain proportions; no Sahara-style endless orange sea, fantasy peaks, storm drama or poster sunset
- photo 6 : quiet watercolor 4; refined painterly realism, visibly illustrated on inspection
- no literal stock-photo look, no heavy watercolor wash
- no text, labels, flag, collage, border, map or UI

Why this Hero:
- dunes + mountain wall + Bactrian-camel scale create a Mongolia-specific landscape rather than a generic open steppe
- the horizontal layers survive desktop and mobile crop well
- the 8 Scenes then deliberately broaden the country into pasture, lake, glacier, city, archaeology and wildlife

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **ゲルは、移動する暮らしに合わせて分解し、組み直せる住まい**
   - UNESCO intangible heritage recognises traditional craftsmanship of the Mongol ger and its associated customs.
   - Explain structure, felt covering and communal making without romanticising nomadism as unchanged.

2. **ホーミーは、一人の声から低い持続音と高い倍音を同時に響かせる**
   - UNESCO identifies Khöömei as a Mongolian vocal tradition with roots particularly in western Mongolia / Altai.
   - Keep the focus on how landscape imitation and sound culture are connected.

3. **ナーダムでは、競馬・相撲・弓射が一つの祭りに集まる**
   - UNESCO: the festival is held nationwide and links the three games with nomadic civilisation.
   - Avoid reducing it to sports results; the communal gathering is the point.

4. **アイラグは、馬乳を発酵させる技術と季節の牧畜がつながる**
   - UNESCO intangible heritage inscription on airag making and related customs.
   - Treat it as a living food practice, not a novelty drink.

5. **遊牧は「定住しないこと」ではなく、季節・牧草・家畜に合わせた移動の体系**
   - UNESCO inscribed Mongol nomad migration and associated practices in 2024.
   - Use this to explain why road / distance / pasture belong together on the page.

6. **伝統モンゴル文字は、縦に連なる文字そのものが文化景観になる**
   - UNESCO Mongolian calligraphy inscription provides the cultural anchor.
   - Keep script history distinct from the Country Profile's simple language fact.

## Travel Scale

### 3〜4日 / city
**ウランバートルから近郊の草原へ出る**

首都の寺院と街を見た後、HustaiやGorkhi-Terelj方面へ短く移動すると、都市と草原の距離感をつかみやすい。  
例：ウランバートル → フスタイ → ゴルヒ・テレルジ → ウランバートル。

### 7〜10日 / map
**古都とゴビを一つの陸路でつなぐ**

Kharkhorin / Orkhon Valleyから南へ回り、BayanzagとKhongoryn Elsまで進む。移動距離が大きいため、地点数より道路条件と一地点の滞在時間を優先する。  
例：ウランバートル → カラコルム → オルホン渓谷 → バヤンザグ → ホンゴリン・エルス → ウランバートル。

### 14日以上 / compass
**北の湖か西のアルタイを、別の地域ブロックとして加える**

遠隔地は「ついで」に足さず、KhövsgölまたはBayan-Ölgii / Altaiのどちらかを一つの旅として組み込む。国内線と陸路を組み合わせ、移動日にも余白を置く。  
例：ウランバートル → カラコルム／オルホン → ムルン → フブスグル湖 → ウランバートル。

The third bucket remains open-ended. A full Gobi + Khövsgöl + Altai circuit should not be implied as comfortable within a fixed upper limit.

## Seasons

1. **4〜5月**
   - dry, windy shoulder season; grassland green-up is uneven
   - large daily temperature ranges remain possible
   - do not promise lush steppe imagery this early

2. **6〜8月**
   - warmest period for broad overland travel
   - central and northern grasslands are generally greener
   - Naadam adds a July cultural marker, while Gobi heat and visitor concentration require planning

3. **9〜10月**
   - clear, dry-feeling autumn light can suit steppe, Gobi and Altai travel
   - nights become cold quickly, especially at altitude and in the north
   - remote services become less forgiving as the season closes

4. **11〜3月**
   - severe continental winter changes road, daylight and accommodation conditions
   - Ulaanbaatar and specialist winter travel remain possible, but this is not a generic first-trip season
   - do not equate “blue sky” with mild conditions

## Transport

Title: **4WD・国内線・鉄道**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
- the large country scale makes transport part of the Mongolia experience
- paved highways connect major corridors, but many scenic routes still involve long unpaved sections; route plans should not treat map distance like European driving time
- 4WD / supported road travel materially improves access to Gobi, Orkhon and remote archaeological landscapes
- domestic flights are important when adding the far west or distant northern regions to a shorter trip
- the central railway corridor links Ulaanbaatar with the China and Russia directions, but current international passenger schedules must be verified before final JSON
- exact flight, train, border and road timings are not evergreen Country-page copy

## Primary Themes

Editorial recommendation:

- **earth / 地球の風景**
- **life / 暮らしに出会う**
- **road / 道の先へ**

Rationale:
- earth is a strong destination motive because Gobi dunes, northern lake / taiga, Altai glaciers and steppe are not background scenery
- life remains central through ger culture, seasonal pastoral movement, horses, food and festivals
- road qualifies at destination level because distance and overland movement materially shape how the country is experienced, rather than being a single scenic-road feature

Do not add `history` simply because Mongolia has major historical sites. History is important content, but the stronger country-selection motives for this page are landscape, nomadic life and movement.

Theme assignment remains exclusively in `data/theme-taxonomy.json`. Mongolia is already assigned to `life`; `earth` and `road` are proposed additions during implementation. Do not duplicate theme assignments in Country JSON.
