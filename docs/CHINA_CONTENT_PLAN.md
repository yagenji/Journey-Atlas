## Page concept

**CHINA / 中国**

Lead direction:

山稜を走る万里の長城から、北京の宮殿、上海の高層建築、湖南の石柱群、チベット高原、福建の土楼、敦煌の砂丘、雲南の棚田、西安の兵馬俑へ。長い距離を移動するほど、地形、都市、建築、食、暮らしの地域差が大きく切り替わる国。

The page should reveal China through:
- the Great Wall as the Hero because it is the most immediately recognisable China-specific landscape marker for a global audience
- Xi'an and the Terracotta Army as a distinct archaeological / imperial-history layer replacing the former Great Wall Scene
- Beijing's imperial axis and Shanghai's contemporary river city as two different urban layers
- Wulingyuan's quartz-sandstone landforms
- the Tibetan Plateau through the Potala Palace in its real Lhasa valley setting
- Fujian tulou as communal earthen architecture embedded in agricultural mountain terrain
- Dunhuang as a Silk Road desert-oasis landscape
- Honghe Hani terraces as a living agricultural landscape shaped by forest, water, villages and cultivation

## Hero

### H — Jinshanling Great Wall / mountain ridge

Working coordinate: **40.6769, 117.2442**  
Planned filename: `hero-jinshanling-great-wall.webp`  
Content ID: `jinshanling-great-wall-hero`

Visual identity:
- real Jinshanling section of the Great Wall
- unmistakable long stone wall with multiple watchtowers visibly following steep, layered mountain ridges
- broad horizontal composition with the wall entering from foreground / lower-left and receding strongly toward centre-right
- enough wall mass and recognisable watchtowers to read instantly as the Great Wall even at mobile crop
- restrained early-autumn greens and ochres under soft clear daylight; slight atmospheric depth in distant ridges
- photo 6 : quiet watercolor 4; refined painterly realism, visibly illustrated on inspection
- no giant crowds, souvenir stalls, flag display, impossible drone altitude, fantasy mountains, exaggerated wall width, dramatic poster sunset or heavy fog hiding the wall
- no text, labels, border, collage, map or UI

Why this Hero:
- the Great Wall is more universally recognisable as China than the Li River and directly answers the requirement that the country be identifiable at first glance
- the long diagonal wall creates a strong horizontal Hero composition while leaving quieter sky / distant-ridge space for copy
- using Jinshanling preserves real mountain context and avoids turning the Hero into a monument close-up

## Travel Scale

### 4〜5日 / city
**北京と長城から、北方の中国を読む**

故宮と北京の都市軸を歩き、郊外の長城へ出る。短い旅では都市と山地を一つの地域ブロックとして深く見る。  
例：北京 → 故宮／景山 → 金山嶺または慕田峪 → 北京。

### 8〜12日 / rail
**北京・西安・上海を高速鉄道でつなぐ**

帝都、古都、現代都市を一本の都市軸として移動し、地域ごとの食と街の違いも見る。移動だけで日程を埋めず、各都市に複数泊置く。  
例：北京 → 西安 → 上海。

### 14日以上 / compass
**一つの遠隔地域ブロックを加える**

桂林／陽朔、四川、雲南、湖南などから一地域を選び、主要都市周遊とは別の景観ブロックとして組み込む。8景を一度に回る旅程は想定しない。  
例：北京 → 西安 → 成都 → 桂林／陽朔 → 上海。

The last bucket remains open-ended because China is too large for a meaningful fixed upper limit.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - spring is useful for many city itineraries, but north / west can remain dry and windy while the south becomes wetter
   - do not imply one nationwide spring climate

2. **6〜8月**
   - eastern and southern lowlands can be hot and humid; plateau and high-mountain regions behave differently
   - rainfall / monsoon patterns matter for southern and mountain landscapes

3. **9〜11月**
   - often a practical broad travel window for combining cities and landscapes
   - autumn timing differs sharply by latitude and altitude; avoid promising uniform foliage

4. **12〜2月**
   - north and inland areas can be cold and dry while parts of the south remain much milder
   - high plateau and desert travel require separate winter planning
   - Lunar New Year travel demand varies by annual calendar and belongs in current practical guidance

## Transport

Title: **高速鉄道・国内線・都市交通**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
- high-speed rail is a major way to connect Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an and many eastern / central cities
- far-western, southwestern and plateau destinations may require domestic flights or long additional overland sectors
- metros and local rail are important inside major cities
- scenic areas such as Wulingyuan, rural tulou districts, terraces and desert sites require last-mile road / bus / transfer planning
- do not turn the national rail-network length into a claim that every Scene is easy to reach by train
