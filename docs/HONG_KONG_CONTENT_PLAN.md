## Page concept

**HONG KONG / 香港**

Lead direction:

高密度の街と港だけでなく、尾根、地質、湿地、客家の集落、漁村、離島までを短い距離の中で行き来する。ヴィクトリア・ハーバーを入口に、香港島・九龍・新界・大嶼山・離島へ視線を広げ、都市と自然が接している地理そのものを見せる。

The page should reveal Hong Kong through:
- Victoria Harbour as the geographic hinge between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon
- a dense street life that remains readable at human scale through trams, markets and neighbourhood movement
- steep ridges and protected countryside immediately behind urban districts
- volcanic geology on the eastern New Territories coast
- wetlands and migratory-bird habitat around Inner Deep Bay
- fishing-village and island communities whose scale differs sharply from Central / Kowloon
- Hakka village history in the northeastern New Territories
- Cantonese food and living intangible heritage as part of everyday culture

Editorial boundary:
- Do not frame Hong Kong as a sovereign state. Hong Kong is a Special Administrative Region of the People’s Republic of China.
- JOURNEY ATLAS treats Hong Kong as an independent travel destination for discoverability only, following `data/atlas-scope.json`.
- Where political-status wording matters, use “香港”, “地域”, or “香港特別行政区”; do not call it an independent country.
- Do not invent a capital. Hong Kong has no separate national capital; the profile should state this explicitly.
- Keep current political commentary outside the travel-atlas editorial scope unless it is directly necessary for a verified travel note.

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **港は景色ではなく、街の骨格になっている**
   - Victoria Harbour separates Hong Kong Island and Kowloon while ferries, rail tunnels and road crossings stitch the two sides together.

2. **高層都市の背後に、法定保護された緑地が大きく残る**
   - Around 40% of land is country parks / special areas.
   - Explain the rapid city-to-ridge transition rather than treating nature as a side trip.

3. **香港の地質には巨大な火山活動の痕跡が残る**
   - Geopark’s rhyolitic hexagonal columns extend across land and sea over 100 km².
   - Avoid implying active volcanism.

4. **新界には、都市化以前の村落文化が現在の景観に残る**
   - Lai Chi Wo and other Hakka / clan villages show settlement forms, woodland management and ritual spaces beyond the harbour core.

5. **祭りや工芸は、観光用の演出だけではなく地域の継承文化**
   - Current Hong Kong Representative List includes Cantonese opera, Cheung Chau Jiao Festival, Tai O dragon boat water parade, Tai Hang fire dragon dance, milk-tea making and others.

6. **フェリーに乗ると、都市の速度が短時間で変わる**
   - Cheung Chau / Lantau / other islands show how water transport is still part of spatial experience, not only a sightseeing cruise.

## Seasons

1. **1〜2月** — 比較的涼しく、時に寒い日もある。尾根歩きや市街地散策を組みやすい。
2. **3〜4月** — 温暖だが湿度が上がり、霧が出ることがある。
3. **5〜8月** — 暑く湿潤。大雨・雷雨が増える。
4. **9〜12月** — 秋は比較的晴天・乾燥が多く、街・島・尾根を組み合わせやすい。熱帯低気圧は主に6〜10月に影響し得る。

## Transport

Title: **MTR・バス・フェリー・トラム**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
- MTR is the core urban network.
- Ferries are part of the geography, especially Victoria Harbour and outlying islands.
- Tram is a slow north-Hong-Kong-Island layer rather than a region-wide transport solution.
- Country parks, Mai Po and remote villages require route-specific bus / minibus / taxi / walking combinations; do not promise one uniform access method.
- Final page must verify current operational details rather than hard-code brittle timetables.

## Travel Notes
