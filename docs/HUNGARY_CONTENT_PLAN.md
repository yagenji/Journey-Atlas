## Page concept

**HUNGARY / ハンガリー**

Lead direction:

ドナウをはさんで丘のブダと平地のペストが向き合う首都から、バラトン湖、東の大平原、北東の葡萄畑と洞窟、西の温泉湖へ。平坦な国土の中に、水・都市・農村の景観がゆるやかに切り替わる中欧の国。

The page should reveal Hungary through:
- Budapest as a Danube city rather than a stand-alone monument collection
- the Danube Bend as the river leaving the capital
- Lake Balaton and the volcanic Tihany Peninsula
- the Puszta / Great Hungarian Plain
- Tokaj's living vineyard and cellar landscape
- the Aggtelek karst underground world
- a preserved but living rural settlement at Hollókő
- Pécs as a visible Roman / Ottoman / Christian historical layer
- Lake Hévíz as a western thermal-water landscape

Editorial balance:
- 1 capital Hero
- 2 water landscapes
- 1 great plain
- 1 wine landscape
- 1 underground natural scene
- 1 rural settlement
- 1 southern historic city
- 1 thermal lake
- no scene is selected simply because it is famous

Working viewpoint coordinate: **47.50639, 19.03861** — Batthyány tér / Buda bank  
Subject coordinate: **47.50726, 19.04536** — Hungarian Parliament Building

Scene identity:
- real view from the Buda side of the Danube near Batthyány tér
- Hungarian Parliament Building clearly identifiable across the river
- wide Danube foreground establishes Budapest as two banks facing one another
- Parliament placed around the right third rather than dead centre
- open sky / quieter left side reserved for Hero text crop

- calm water, low visual noise, no oversized boats
- Gothic Revival Parliament proportions must remain plausible

- Parliament is immediately recognizable as Hungary
- the Danube explains the urban geography of Buda and Pest at first glance
- a riverbank viewpoint survives desktop and mobile crop better than a Fisherman's Bastion arch-framed composition

## Signature Facts

Exactly 3:

1. **標高200m未満の国土 — 82.5%**
   - KSH geographic data show 82.5% of the country lies below 200 m.
   - This is more useful for understanding Hungary than a generic highest-point fact.

2. **ブダペストの温泉源・井戸 — 100超**
   - Official tourism information states Budapest has more than 100 thermal springs and wells, with thermal water reaching up to 77°C.
   - This gives a concrete scale to the capital's bath culture.

3. **トカイの閉鎖ワイン地域 — 1737年**
   - UNESCO records the 1737 legal delimitation of the Tokaj wine region as among the world's earliest such delimitations.
   - Use this to connect vineyards, cellars and long-running land use rather than simply counting wine regions.

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **二千年以上つながる温泉文化**
   - Roman bathing, Ottoman baths and 19th-century bath architecture overlap in Budapest and elsewhere.
   - Official tourism sources describe more than 2,000 years of bathing history in the capital.

2. **タンツハーズは「保存」より、踊って伝える仕組み**
   - The Táncház dance-house model combines direct observation / imitation with live music and modern teaching.
   - UNESCO selected it in 2011 for the Register of Good Safeguarding Practices.

3. **弦楽バンドが村の祝いと踊りを支えてきた**
   - Fiddle / viola / bass-based ensembles have played at village, family, agricultural, religious and public occasions.
   - UNESCO inscribed the Hungarian string band tradition in 2022.

4. **マチョー刺繍は衣装だけでなく共同体の表現**
   - Floral embroidery around Mezőkövesd appears on festive dress, objects and contemporary applications.
   - UNESCO inscribed the tradition in 2012.

5. **モハーチでは冬の終わりに木の仮面のブショーが街を歩く**
   - The six-day late-February Busó festivities combine masks, procession, music and communal participation.
   - UNESCO inscribed the custom in 2009.

6. **M1は1896年から街の地下を走る**
   - The Millennium Underground Railway was built in 1893–96 and opened in 1896.
   - UNESCO identifies it as the first underground railway in continental Europe.

Keep these distinct from Scene descriptions, Signature Facts and Taste.

## Travel Trivia

Exactly 5:

1. **ブダペストは1873年にブダ・ペスト・オーブダが一つの都市になった**
   - Use UNESCO's 1872–73 unification history; avoid simplified “Buda + Pest only” wording.

2. **ティハニ修道院の1055年の創建文書には、現存する初期のハンガリー語が残る**
   - The founding charter is preserved in original form and includes Hungarian words inside a Latin text.

3. **ヘーヴィーズ湖の水は約3日で入れ替わる**
   - Official tourism information attributes this to the lake's abundant thermal-water supply.
   - Lake area: 4.4 ha; depth: 38 m.

4. **ヴィシェグラードでは1335年に三王会談が開かれた**
   - The meeting brought together the Hungarian, Czech and Polish kings and later gave its name to the Visegrád regional grouping.

5. **ハンガリーには世界遺産が8件ある**
   - UNESCO currently lists eight World Heritage properties for Hungary.
   - Use as a compact country-level fact, not as a scene-selection rule.

## Travel Scale

### 3〜4日 / city
**ブダペストを軸にドナウの上流へ**  
首都の両岸を歩き、日帰りまたは1泊でセンテンドレ／ヴィシェグラードを加える程度なら、都市と大河の地形を無理なくつなげられる。例：ブダペスト → ヴィシェグラード → ブダペスト。

### 5〜7日 / map
**首都から北東の城・葡萄畑へつなぐ**  
鉄道とバスを組み合わせ、ドナウから北東部へ広げる。例：ブダペスト → エゲル → トカイ → ブダペスト。自然を加える場合はアッグテレクで1泊分の余裕を取る。

### 8日以上 / compass
**湖・南部都市・大平原まで横断する**  
公共交通だけでなく車も組み合わせると、国土の東西差を急がず追える。例：ブダペスト → ティハニ／バラトン湖 → ヘーヴィーズ → ペーチ → ホルトバージ → トカイ／アッグテレク。

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - Budapest and lower-country walking becomes comfortable as vegetation returns.
   - Balaton, Danube Bend and rural landscapes can be combined before peak summer heat.
   - Northern hills / caves remain cooler; check local opening and weather conditions.

2. **6〜8月**
   - Lake Balaton and water-based travel fit naturally into the route.
   - Budapest and the Great Plain can become hot; use mornings / evenings for exposed city and plain landscapes.
   - Summer thunderstorms can be locally strong.

3. **9〜10月**
   - Tokaj harvest-season landscape and early autumn colour around the Danube Bend become strong visual reasons to travel.
   - Temperatures are generally easier for city / vineyard / plain combinations than midsummer.

4. **11〜2月**
   - Short daylight and cold continental weather shift the balance toward Budapest, thermal baths and historic cities.
   - Hévíz steam is visually characteristic in cool weather.
   - Rural roads, cave schedules and occasional winter conditions need more planning margin.

## Transport

Title: **鉄道・都市間バス・車**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
Budapest is the national rail hub and regional trains connect major cities and Lake Balaton. Intercity buses extend coverage to smaller towns. Budapest, Pécs, Balaton and much of the Danube Bend can be planned with public transport, while a route combining Aggtelek, Hortobágy viewpoints, cellar landscapes and several rural scenes becomes materially easier with a rental car or planned transfers.

Do not copy old ticket prices or timetable minutes from evergreen tourism pages into production copy; verify current operator information if exact service details are needed.

   - Budapest and Pécs make Roman, medieval, Ottoman and 19th-century layers visible within living cities.

   - The strongest geographic contrast appears when the route leaves Budapest for the Puszta, Tokaj, Aggtelek and Hollókő.

## Travel Notes

Exactly 3:

1. **ブダペストの移動時間と地方の移動時間を同じ感覚で組まない**
   - The capital has dense public transport, while rural World Heritage / landscape sites may require transfers or a car.

2. **夏の大平原と都市歩きは暑さを前提に時間帯を分ける**
   - Exposed plains and stone-paved urban areas are better scheduled with midday heat in mind.

3. **洞窟・温泉・ワイン地域は目的地ごとの利用条件を事前確認する**
   - Cave tours, bath access, wine tastings and seasonal facilities can have reservation, schedule or access constraints.

## Primary Themes

Editorial recommendation:
- **city / 街を歩く**
- **history / 時をたどる**
- **food / 食をめぐる**

Rationale:
- Budapest is itself a primary travel motivation, with a walkable river-city structure and major architecture.
- Roman, medieval, Ottoman and Austro-Hungarian layers are unusually visible across Budapest / Pécs / Visegrád / rural heritage.
- Paprika-based cuisine, street food, confectionery and Tokaj wine provide enough depth to be a destination-level motivation rather than a secondary tag.

Do **not** assign `earth` merely because Hungary has plains, caves and lakes. Natural scenery is important to page diversity but is not as strong a destination-choice driver as city / history / food.

Theme assignment remains exclusively in `data/theme-taxonomy.json`; it must not be duplicated in the future Country JSON.
