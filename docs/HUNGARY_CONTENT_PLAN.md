## Page concept

**HUNGARY / ハンガリー**

Lead direction:

ドナウをはさんで丘のブダと平地のペストが向き合う首都から、バラトン湖、東の大平原、北東の葡萄畑と洞窟、西の温泉湖へ。平坦な国土の中に、水・都市・農村の景観がゆるやかに切り替わる中欧の国。

The page should reveal Hungary through:
- Budapest as a Danube city rather than a stand-alone monument collection
- the Danube Bend as the river leaving the capital
- Lake Balaton and the volcanic Tihany Peninsula
- the Puszta / Great Hungarian Plain
- Tokaj's living vineyard and cellar landscape
- the Aggtelek karst underground world
- a preserved but living rural settlement at Hollókő
- Pécs as a visible Roman / Ottoman / Christian historical layer
- Lake Hévíz as a western thermal-water landscape

Editorial balance:
- 1 capital Hero
- 2 water landscapes
- 1 great plain
- 1 wine landscape
- 1 underground natural scene
- 1 rural settlement
- 1 southern historic city
- 1 thermal lake
- no scene is selected simply because it is famous

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **二千年以上つながる温泉文化**
   - Roman bathing, Ottoman baths and 19th-century bath architecture overlap in Budapest and elsewhere.
   - Official tourism sources describe more than 2,000 years of bathing history in the capital.

2. **タンツハーズは「保存」より、踊って伝える仕組み**
   - The Táncház dance-house model combines direct observation / imitation with live music and modern teaching.
   - UNESCO selected it in 2011 for the Register of Good Safeguarding Practices.

3. **弦楽バンドが村の祝いと踊りを支えてきた**
   - Fiddle / viola / bass-based ensembles have played at village, family, agricultural, religious and public occasions.
   - UNESCO inscribed the Hungarian string band tradition in 2022.

4. **マチョー刺繍は衣装だけでなく共同体の表現**
   - Floral embroidery around Mezőkövesd appears on festive dress, objects and contemporary applications.
   - UNESCO inscribed the tradition in 2012.

5. **モハーチでは冬の終わりに木の仮面のブショーが街を歩く**
   - The six-day late-February Busó festivities combine masks, procession, music and communal participation.
   - UNESCO inscribed the custom in 2009.

6. **M1は1896年から街の地下を走る**
   - The Millennium Underground Railway was built in 1893–96 and opened in 1896.
   - UNESCO identifies it as the first underground railway in continental Europe.

Keep these distinct from Scene descriptions, Signature Facts and Taste.

## Travel Scale

### 3〜4日 / city
**ブダペストを軸にドナウの上流へ**  
首都の両岸を歩き、日帰りまたは1泊でセンテンドレ／ヴィシェグラードを加える程度なら、都市と大河の地形を無理なくつなげられる。例：ブダペスト → ヴィシェグラード → ブダペスト。

### 5〜7日 / map
**首都から北東の城・葡萄畑へつなぐ**  
鉄道とバスを組み合わせ、ドナウから北東部へ広げる。例：ブダペスト → エゲル → トカイ → ブダペスト。自然を加える場合はアッグテレクで1泊分の余裕を取る。

### 8日以上 / compass
**湖・南部都市・大平原まで横断する**  
公共交通だけでなく車も組み合わせると、国土の東西差を急がず追える。例：ブダペスト → ティハニ／バラトン湖 → ヘーヴィーズ → ペーチ → ホルトバージ → トカイ／アッグテレク。

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - Budapest and lower-country walking becomes comfortable as vegetation returns.
   - Balaton, Danube Bend and rural landscapes can be combined before peak summer heat.
   - Northern hills / caves remain cooler; check local opening and weather conditions.

2. **6〜8月**
   - Lake Balaton and water-based travel fit naturally into the route.
   - Budapest and the Great Plain can become hot; use mornings / evenings for exposed city and plain landscapes.
   - Summer thunderstorms can be locally strong.

3. **9〜10月**
   - Tokaj harvest-season landscape and early autumn colour around the Danube Bend become strong visual reasons to travel.
   - Temperatures are generally easier for city / vineyard / plain combinations than midsummer.

4. **11〜2月**
   - Short daylight and cold continental weather shift the balance toward Budapest, thermal baths and historic cities.
   - Hévíz steam is visually characteristic in cool weather.
   - Rural roads, cave schedules and occasional winter conditions need more planning margin.
