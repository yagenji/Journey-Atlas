OSM-derived exceptions are ODbL; the shared `assets/js/map-source-credit.js` and existing focused browser test are the attribution route. A source name alone is not a geographic verification. Before any Stage ③ action, recalculate the roster and exact source hashes from the latest main and the PR head; those can advance independently.

## Geographic QA still open

**Bahrain:** original approved source `assets/images/bahrain/map-atlas-v3.svg`, not v1. The Hawar inset's generated five GSHHG rings were compared with the approved Qatar and Bahrain paths. Two rings match Qatar; three overlap approved Hawar geometry, so the migration-only adapter now keeps only the two verified Qatar rings, with exact input-path/hash guards. This resolves one provenance ambiguity but **does not** resolve the western-edge pale wedge, mainland coast interface or all Hawar source-vintage discrepancies. The SLRB 2024 1:100,000 national map and 1:50,000 Hawar reference exist, but no redistributable matching vector or compatible full topology is verified. No arbitrary ring deletion.

**Hong Kong:** the current candidate shows disconnected Shenzhen/New Territories pale mainland fragments and apparent sea gaps. All 18 approved target paths must remain exact; HKSAR Lands Department data alone does not certify adjoining Mainland polygon topology or its redistribution rights. Do not bridge fragments by guessed lines or fill.

**Brunei:** the combined generated land ring contains genuine Malaysian land as well as a near-complete overlap of Brunei's two approved parts; dropping it deletes Malaysia, promoting it unchanged leaves misleading seams. Source-backed Malaysia/Brunei coast-and-border reconciliation is needed; overlap-based subtraction by itself is not authoritative.

**Monaco:** the gross GSHHG diagonal is not a coast. Both 2018 simplified IGN-derived French commune polygons and a 2025 IGN Admin Express comparison remove that diagonal, but residual coastline/border seams against the protected Monaco OSM path persist. Do not smooth, pad or fill by inference; preserve the comparative sources and their licensing from the [historical detailed record](https://github.com/yagenji/Journey-Atlas/blob/54c8c7f26abbfc4f25264b65f529cef693ab9825/docs/MAP_CONTEXT_ROLLOUT.md).

**Portugal:** the three mainland/Azores/Madeira viewports have been framed to explain genuine clipping. This is a presentation fix, not a check that Spain and island/inset geometry are source-compatible. Independent native-size mask comparison on the staged SVG found: mainland target 111,437 px, GSHHG context 209,739 px with 110,935 px overlap; Azores target 865 px, context 781 px with 747 px overlap and 34 px outside; Madeira target 2,486 px, context 153 px with 105 px overlap and 48 px outside. This **identifies duplicated own-island geometry in both ocean insets**, but 34/48 outlying pixels have not been assigned a country from independent licensed data. An uncommitted proof-of-concept removing the Azores/Madeira *generated context paths only* preserved all 15 approved national paths and fully decoded at 1200×760. No foreign coastal polygon has been accepted or deleted on the PR; the mainland Spain coast still needs native-size source alignment review. No blanket PASS.

**Qatar/Kuwait:** reviewed Doha and Kuwait City generated self-land has been removed without editing protected target paths; national-scale coastline alignment, source terms and finished-map geography are not thereby approved.

**Singapore/Macau:** reconciled OSM neighboring-land geometry, original national-path parity and tested visible OSM attribution address specific city-scale defects. Mismatched source vintages at cross-border coasts still require independent full-size signoff; existing input-source and output-path hashes are necessary, not sufficient.

**Antigua & Barbuda / Malta:** removal of generated self-island duplicates and Malta's no-foreign-land no-op do not establish the accuracy of every residual neighboring polygon. Preserve approved country paths.

**Remaining candidates, including El Salvador:** each map needs full-size geographic review with independent coast/border provenance and an individual disposition. A fresh scan of the 94 stored candidates found 28 with at least one generated polygon edge longer than 60 SVG px entirely inside the canvas, including El Salvador. This is a **triage signal, not proof of error**: true inland boundaries and valid low-detail coasts can have long segments. El Salvador's native-size preview shows several small pale polygon slivers away from the target coastline; their ownership has not been established. Neither a four-contact-sheet scan nor SHA/PNG/browser QA can confer a PASS on the other countries. Keep all geographically unverified candidates on HOLD.

## Stage gates

**Stage ②:** For every selected map, record source name/version/license and actual geographic extent, independently check each native 1200×760 composition (mainland, islands, exclaves, adjacent land, all insets), compare source vintages and the approved national paths, verify exact path/marker/source SHA preservation, and resolve or explicitly obtain approval for any change in scope. A rejected candidate is an unresolved requested map, **not** a geographic PASS or a completed rollout. Test affected real pages across Desktop / Tablet / Mobile and source credit. The stager `scripts/stage_map_context_rollout.py` does not exclude rejected candidates and its batch artifact is only for read-only QA: every entry now explicitly records `geographicQaStatus: "HOLD"` and `promotionEligible: false`; the existing map-context preflight asserts both values. **This is not a mechanism for geographic approval or production promotion.**

**Stage ③:** Only after Stage ② source-backed disposition and approval for the entire requested roster; regenerate exact current-main roster and SHA; implement reviewed changes on the draft branch, then verify exact affected assets, real pages, markers, accessibility, licenses and unchanged publication. Do not silently omit a country.

**Stage ④:** Only after explicit user final approval: merge/deploy and verify deployed SHA, live page URLs, navigation, `atlasPublished`, robots/noindex and sitemap. Never publish an unapproved Country.
