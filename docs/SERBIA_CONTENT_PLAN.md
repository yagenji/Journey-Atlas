## Page concept

**SERBIA / セルビア**

Lead direction:
大河の街から、深い峡谷と山の修道院へ。北の平原と南の山地を進むほど、セルビアの時間の層が見えてくる。

The page should reveal Serbia through:
- Danube/Sava river cities and fortresses
- Vojvodina's Central European urban culture
- medieval Orthodox heritage
- Roman-era sites
- western/eastern/southern mountain and gorge landscapes
- living culture, family customs, crafts and food

4. **コヴァチツァのナイーヴ絵画** — minority/rural life represented through a distinctive local painting tradition.

## Travel Trivia

Target 5:
1. **Petrovaradin's reversed clock** — the large hand shows hours and the small hand minutes.
2. **冬の祝祭暦** — Orthodox Christmas is celebrated on 7 January; the Serbian New Year follows in mid-January.
3. **スラトコと水** — sweet fruit preserve with cold water is a small everyday hospitality tradition travelers may encounter.
4. **市場で地域差を見る** — regional foods such as Leskovac ajvar and Futog cabbage appear as place-linked products.
5. **「Živeli!」で乾杯** — a useful phrase to recognize and join a toast without turning the page into a phrasebook.

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月** — green landscapes and city walking; mountain conditions remain variable.
2. **6〜8月** — rivers, Uvac and Tara; hot lowlands make mountain areas useful counterpoints.
3. **9〜11月** — forest color and comfortable early-autumn walking; later autumn becomes cooler/wetter.
4. **12〜2月** — winter city travel plus mountain snow/skiing; road and mountain access need more margin.

## Transport

Title: **高速鉄道・バス・車**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
The Belgrade–Novi Sad–Subotica corridor is well suited to rail. Intercity buses cover many cities and towns. Uvac, Tara, Đerdap viewpoints and dispersed monastery/nature sites are more practical with a car or carefully planned bus/transfer combinations.

## Travel Notes

Exactly 3:
1. **ウヴァツは保護区の訪問方法を事前確認する**
   - Guided visits and access rules can matter; confirm the current arrangement before travel.

2. **北部の鉄道と山地の移動を同じ感覚で組まない**
   - Fast rail is strong on the northern corridor, while mountain/gorge sites need more transfer and driving time.

3. **山へ入る日は季節と道路条件を見る**
   - Winter snow and shoulder-season conditions can change access; allow margin for Tara, Kopaonik and other mountain areas.
