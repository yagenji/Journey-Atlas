## Page concept

**POLAND / ポーランド**

Lead direction:
ヴィスワ川沿いの王都から、再建された首都、バルト海の港、湖沼地帯、移動砂丘、タトラの高山へ。都市の歴史と北から南へ変わる地形をたどると、ポーランドの広さが見えてくる。

The page should reveal Poland through:
- Kraków and Warsaw as two different historical city narratives
- Baltic trade and port-city heritage in Gdańsk
- medieval brick architecture at Malbork
- alpine landscape in the Polish Tatras
- the moving dune landscape of the Baltic coast
- the lake-and-sailing culture of Masuria
- underground industrial/religious space at Wieliczka
- western Polish urban identity in Wrocław
- living culture such as milk bars, amber, ceramics, music and local craft

## Beyond the Scenery

Exactly 6:

1. **ワルシャワ旧市街の再建**
   - More than 85% of the historic centre was destroyed in 1944; rebuilding became a rare conservation case recognized by UNESCO.

2. **グダニスクと「連帯」**
   - The 1980 shipyard strike and Solidarity movement connect the waterfront city to the political transformation of late-20th-century Poland.

3. **クラクフのショプカ**
   - Miniature nativity constructions transform Kraków's real architecture into highly decorated hand-made scenes; UNESCO intangible heritage since 2018.

4. **ポロネーズ**
   - The formal processional dance remains a recognizable element of Polish celebrations and cultural identity; now listed by UNESCO as intangible heritage.

5. **ビャウォヴィエジャの原生林とヨーロッパバイソン**
   - Keep this out of the 8 Scenes because a forest image risks reading as generic; use the editorial section to explain why the forest is ecologically exceptional.

6. **ウッチの工場都市**
   - Use the textile-industry city and reuse of brick factory complexes to add an industrial/modern urban layer absent from the scenic selection.

## Travel Trivia

Target 5:

1. **聖マリア教会のラッパは毎時鳴る**
   - Kraków's hejnał is played from the tower each hour and famously stops abruptly.

2. **ワルシャワの人魚は剣と盾を持つ**
   - The armed mermaid is the capital's civic symbol and appears around the Old Town and riverfront.

3. **ヴロツワフでは小さなブロンズの小人を探せる**
   - Avoid a fixed count because new figures are added; treat the citywide search itself as the travel experience.

4. **移動砂丘は、埋もれた森の痕跡を現す**
   - Słowiński's moving dunes can uncover old tree stumps as the dune belt advances.

5. **「脂の木曜日」はポンチキの日**
   - Use this as a seasonal food custom, distinct from the basic Taste description.

## Taste

Proposed four dishes:

1. **ピエロギ・ルスキ / Pierogi ruskie**
   - potato-and-white-cheese filled dumplings; visual identity = crescent dumplings on one plain plate
   - note in final copy that “ruskie” refers historically to Ruthenia, not Russia, if explanation is needed

2. **ジュレック / Żurek**
   - sour rye soup with sausage and egg; one plain bowl only
   - do not use a bread bowl unless the final dish identity specifically requires it

3. **オスツィペク・ズ・ジュラヴィノン / Oscypek z żurawiną**
   - grilled highlander smoked cheese with cranberry accompaniment on the same plate
   - use the characteristic patterned cheese shape

4. **ポンチキ / Pączki**
   - filled Polish doughnut; one simple plate
   - no coffee, napkin, sugar jar or bakery background

Selection rationale:
- dumpling / soup / mountain cheese / pastry
- four clearly different silhouettes and textures
- national + regional balance
- visually stronger than using multiple brown meat/stew dishes

## Seasons

1. **3〜5月**
   - cities become comfortable for walking; mountain trails can still hold snow and require current-condition checks.

2. **6〜8月**
   - strongest season for Masurian sailing, Baltic coast and long daylight; major cities and Morskie Oko are also busiest.

3. **9〜11月**
   - early autumn works well for cities, forests and mountains; later autumn becomes colder, wetter and shorter in daylight.

4. **12〜2月**
   - winter city travel, Christmas-season Kraków/Wrocław and snow in the Tatras; mountain access must follow current weather and trail conditions.

## Transport

Title: **鉄道・バス・車**  
Icon: `road`

Direction:
- rail is the main spine for Warsaw, Kraków, Gdańsk, Wrocław and Malbork
- buses/local transport complete access to Wieliczka and mountain gateways
- a car becomes more useful for Masuria, Słowiński National Park and dispersed rural/nature destinations
- do not imply that every natural Scene is practical as a rail day trip

## Travel Notes

Exactly 3 — final operational details to be refreshed at implementation:

1. **タトラは駐車・入山・トレイル条件を先に確認する**

2. **ヴィエリチカは入場時間を決めてからクラクフの日程へ組み込む**
